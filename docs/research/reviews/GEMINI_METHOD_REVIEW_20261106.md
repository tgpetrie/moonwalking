### 2. Target & Estimand Verdict: Implemented Behavior vs. Unresolved Policy

#### A. Does the Primary Pointwise Target (`verified_forward`) Measure a Useful Future Move at a Decision Cutoff?

Yes—as a fixed-horizon, cutoff-anchored continuation/impulse estimand (`verified_forward(b, H)`), it is mathematically sound, causal, and free of trigger-gate circularity.

* **Implemented Behavior (`scripts/scoring_experiment/instant_targets.py` $\rightarrow$ `TargetEvaluator.evaluate`, Source 12; `scripts/scoring_experiment/instant_association.py` $\rightarrow$ `association_report`, Source 17):**
At any 5s grid decision cutoff $b$ (`anchor_ts_ms`) and horizon $H \in \{300\text{s}, 900\text{s}\}$, `verified_forward` grades whether the signed market-relative residual $u(b+H) = d \cdot [\log(\text{mid}(b+H)/\text{mid}(b)) - \beta(b)\log(\text{BTC}(b+H)/\text{BTC}(b))]$ clears $\max(3\sigma_{1\text{m}}\sqrt{H/60}, 0.015, 3\cdot\text{entry\_spread\_fraction})$, retains $\ge 60\%$ of its $[b, b+H]$ peak (`retention >= 0.6`), and has $\ge 3$ deduplicated forward matches strictly in $(b, b+H]$. In `instant_association.py` (`association_report`), an alert's primary outcome (`pointwise_success_H` / `pointwise_failure_H`) is determined strictly at its declared cutoff $b$ and fixed horizon $H$; retrospective catalog coverage never forgives a pointwise failure (`association_never_forgives_pointwise_failure: true`, Source 16).



#### B. What Additional Evidence Is Needed to Call an Alert "Early Warning" Rather Than "Mid-Move Continuation"?

Because `verified_forward(b, H)` only measures what happens *after* cutoff $b$ (`[b, b+H]`), a high `verified_forward` hit rate proves **forward displacement and retention from $b$**, which is a necessary condition for early warning but **not sufficient on its own** to distinguish early-stage discovery from mid-trend continuation. The current codebase separates four distinct concepts that must not be conflated:

1. **Physical Onset (`physical_onset_defined: false` in Sources 11, 12, 16, 18, 20):** Not defined or implemented. Neither $b$, nor $b-30\text{s}$, nor any catalog anchor identifies the physical first tick of a move.


2. **Earliest Qualifying Forward-Window Anchor (`forward_event_catalog` in Source 12; `forward_catalog_report` in Source 13):** Selects the earliest 5s grid anchor $a$ where `verified_forward(a, H)` is `True` and suppresses `[a, a+H)`. Because an impulse that physically begins at $t_{\text{phys}} = 270\text{s}$ can satisfy the 300s terminal and retention rules for a window anchored at $a = 0\text{s}$, the earliest qualifying forward-window anchor can predate the physical move by up to $H - \Delta t_{\text{impulse}}$. Conversely, if prior grid points were `unavailable`, the catalog anchor is left-censored (`left_censored: True` in `instant_diagnostics.forward_catalog_report`, Source 13).


3. **Strict Activity-Trigger Timing & Whole-Reference-Window Peak (`TargetEvaluator._v2_alert_timing(strict_trigger=True)` in Source 12):** Evaluates alerts relative to the secondary `verified_onset` subset (`before_activity_trigger`, `early_after_activity_trigger`, `late_after_activity_trigger`) using `pre_window_peak_ts_ms` (the maximum residual from $b-30\text{s}$ across $[b-30\text{s}, b+H]$). If a pre-trigger wick in $[b-30\text{s}, b)$ exceeds the subsequent forward peak, `whole_window_peak_precedes_anchor` is `True` (`instant_diagnostics.alert_report`, Source 13) and strict timing classifies an alert at $b$ as `late_after_activity_trigger` because the whole-window peak has already passed.


4. **Separately Named Consumption Fractions (`instant_targets.py` Source 12 & `instant_diagnostics.py` Source 13):**
* `post_anchor_consumed_fraction`: $\frac{u(b \to t_{\text{snap}})}{\max_{t \in [b, b+H]} u(b \to t)}$ (measures consumption strictly after $b$).


* `pre_window_consumed_fraction`: $\frac{u(b-30\text{s} \to t_{\text{snap}})}{\max_{t \in [b-30\text{s}, b+H]} u(b-30\text{s} \to t)}$ (whole-reference-window peak denominator).


* `pre_window_to_forward_peak_consumed_fraction`: $\frac{u(b-30\text{s} \to t_{\text{snap}})}{\max_{t \in [b, b+H]} u(b-30\text{s} \to t)}$ (forward-peak denominator from the $b-30\text{s}$ price reference, implemented in `instant_diagnostics.alert_report`, Source 13).





**How to Establish Early Warning Without Importing Hindsight or Changing Accepted Metrics:**
To demonstrate that the instant radar is providing **early warning** rather than chasing extended moves—without inventing a retrospective physical-onset algorithm or altering the accepted metrics—require three joint pieces of evidence in the prospective study:

* **Causal Pre-Alert Extension & Run-Up Stratification:** Because live candidates must pass the hard causal `EXTENDED` exclusion (`E <= 3.5` and not `[abs(r_24h) > 10% and vol_24h < $1M]`, Source 02 Section 5), additionally report `verified_forward(b, H)` hit rates stratified by causal pre-cutoff 3m/15m run-up deciles (`abs(r_180s)/sigma_1m` and `E`). Genuine early warning must show positive `verified_forward` lift in low-extension strata (`IGNITING` before large 3m displacement has already occurred).


* **Diagnostic Consumption Distribution on Successful Alerts:** Among `pointwise_success_H` alerts, report the empirical distribution of both `pre_window_consumed_fraction` and `pre_window_to_forward_peak_consumed_fraction` (plus `lead_to_forward_peak_seconds`) from `instant_diagnostics.alert_report` (Source 13), verifying that the bulk of net displacement occurs *after* publication (`t_alert`) rather than during `[b-30s, t_alert]`.


* **Pre-Trigger Lead Share on the Secondary Subset:** On the secondary `verified_onset` catalog, report the share of associated alerts classified as `before_activity_trigger` (`publish_ts_ms < anchor`) vs. `early_after_activity_trigger`.

**Cross-Feature Shared Controls & Evaluation Rules:**

#### B. Policy Tradeoffs That Must Be Fixed for Cutoff vs. Publication Timing

Because **a closing heartbeat for cutoff $b$ can never have `available_ts_ms <= b**` (`known = max(processed, receipt + 1)` in `instant_coverage.py` line 183, Source 25; explicitly documented in `INSTANT_INPUT_EVIDENCE_20261104.md`, Source 28), any rule requiring `proof_available_ts_ms <= b` will mark **100% of trade intervals unavailable at $b$**!
To resolve this cleanly without backdating timestamps or altering `TargetEvaluator` (`instant_targets.py`, Source 12), adopt the following two-clock policy:

1. **Strict Separation of Event-Receipt Cutoff ($b$) from Proof/Decision Availability ($t_{\text{avail}} \le b + \Delta_{\text{max}}$):**
* **Proposal:** Freeze **feature observation content** strictly to packets with `receipt_ts_ms < b` (`[b-30s, b)` and `[b-180s, b)`), but evaluate **decision eligibility for cutoff $b$** at `decision_completed_ts_ms` bounded by a predeclared maximum verification/publication budget `max_feature_age_ms` (e.g., **proposed $\Delta_{\text{max}} = 2,500\text{ms}$**, which covers the 1,000ms heartbeat cadence plus the preserved-hour median `965.5ms` / max `1,993ms` race-merge wait in Source 07, while stays strictly inside a single 5,000ms grid step so cutoff $b$'s decision always publishes before cutoff $b+5,000\text{ms}$).
* **Why this avoids backdating:** In `Sample` (`instant_targets.py`, Source 12) and `micro_baseline.py` (Source 14), `available_ts_ms` on historical/offline evaluation rows represents *event receipt availability within the $[b-5\text{s}, b)$ bucket* (`receipt_ts_ms < b`), whereas the asynchronous heartbeat proof completion (`inputs_available_ts_ms > b`) is tracked explicitly in `PublicationTiming` (`instant_publication.py`, Source 21) and `instant_association._alerts()` (`anchor <= inputs_available <= published <= asof`, Source 17).




2. **Cutoff-Anchored Primary Target vs. Publication-Anchored Executable Diagnostic:**
* **Retain `verified_forward(b, H)` at Grid Cutoff $b$ as the Primary Pointwise Target (`INSTANT_ASSOCIATION_CONTRACT_V1_DRAFT.json`, Source 16):** Keeps all grid points on the uniform 5s lattice and preserves exact comparability across rankers evaluated at the same cutoff $b$.


* **Add a Separate Publication-Anchored Return Diagnostic for Published Alerts:** For any published alert (`published_ts_ms` $= t_{\text{alert}}$), record the **first post-publication quote** (`receipt_ts_ms >= t_alert`, within $10\text{s}$) alongside the last pre-publication quote (`ts = floor(t_alert / 5000) * 5000`) to report **implementation shortfall / publication slippage bps** ($\text{mid}(t_{\text{alert}}^+) - \text{mid}(b)$) and net-of-cost coin returns over $[t_{\text{alert}}^+, t_{\text{alert}}^+ + H]$ without changing `verified_forward(b, H)`.

### 5. Evaluation, Cohort Selection, Dependence & 14-Day Power Discipline

1. **Per-Direction / Per-Horizon Cohorts & Gradability (`instant_directional_exposure.py`, Source 19):**
* Report four primary tables separately: `(up, 300s)`, `(up, 900s)`, `(down, 300s)`, and `(down, 900s)`.


* Use `instant_directional_exposure.exposure_report()` (Source 19) as the authoritative board exposure denominator: a slot `(product_id, direction, ts_ms)` is `decision_eligible` based strictly on causal flags known by the decision (`universe_member`, `warmup_complete`, `health_verified`, `candidate_eligible`), never conditioned on whether future labels are `ready` (`decision_universe_independent_of_future_labels: True`).


* A `decision_eligible` slot is `gradable` for `(direction, H)` when `ts_ms + H*1000 <= asof_ts_ms` and its **own-direction** label has `status == "ready"` (`opposite_direction_label_required: False`, Source 19). Keep the older joint both-direction gradability table in `instant_association.py` (Source 17) strictly as a secondary diagnostic.




2. **Identical Complete Rows vs. Natural Coverage:**
* Always report both: (a) **Identical Complete Rows** (`eligible_own_direction_ready_and_scored_by_every_ranker`, Source 19) for head-to-head ranker comparisons (Candidate vs. B0 percentage move vs. B1 current live strength), and (b) **Natural Ranker Coverage** (`eligible_scored_slot_count` and `gradable_scored_slot_count` per ranker) so any coverage shrinkage caused by F2's $\ge 5$ matches gate, F3's 30s continuous quote-age gate, or F4's $\ge 10$ traded-candles gate is completely transparent.




3. **False-Alert Burden & Cooldowns:**
* Evaluate Top-$K$ board entries (`K=5` primary, `K=10` descriptive) under the 15m (`900s`) per-product cooldown shared across boards (`LIVE_MOVERS_PROSPECTIVE_CONTRACT_20261027.md` Section 6, Source 02).


* Define **Pointwise False Alert Rate per Board Hour** as $\frac{\text{failure\_count}_H}{\text{board\_wall\_clock\_hours\_with\_any\_eligible\_product}}$, alongside `success_rate_among_graded` ($\frac{\text{success\_count}_H}{\text{success\_count}_H + \text{failure\_count}_H}$) and `ungradable_count` (`instant_association.py`, Source 17; `instant_directional_exposure.py`, Source 19).




4. **Overlap Dependence, Purging, Paired Uncertainty & What 14 Days Can Establish:**
* Enforce a **$\ge 60\text{m}$ purge window** at the end of Day 7 (Discovery) before Day 8 (Validation) begins so no 5m/15m forward labels or trailing cooldowns cross the split (`LIVE_MOVERS_PROSPECTIVE_CONTRACT_20261027.md` Section 6, Source 02).


* Compute confidence intervals via **paired UTC-day block bootstrap** (and 3-day moving-block bootstrap as in `LIVE_MOVERS_RESULTS_20261027.md`, Source 09), resampling entire UTC days with all products, boards, and rankers kept together.


* **What 14 Valid Shadow Days (7 Discovery / 7 Validation) Can and Cannot Establish:** Seven validation days provide only $N=7$ day blocks (or 5 overlapping 3-day blocks). As demonstrated in the historical comparison (`LIVE_MOVERS_RESULTS_20261027.md`, Source 09), where a $+0.01265$ daily IC gain had a 3-day block 95% CI of $[-0.00047, +0.02545]$, 7 validation days can detect large, consistent daily rank-ordering improvements or severe negative failures, but **cannot reliably resolve small marginal effects of individual features across diverse market regimes**. Therefore, readiness minima (minimum deduplicated `forward_event_catalog` episodes and minimum distinct active products per direction/horizon) must be frozen *before* fitting; if Days 1–7 or Days 8–14 do not meet those minima, the protocol must mandate extending collection in predeclared 7-day increments rather than relaxing thresholds or declaring a null-spanning interval "proof of no effect".

### 7. Summary of Accepted Choices: Retained, Qualified, or Recommended for Amendment

Every distinction below separates **accepted/verified implementation** from **design preference or open study policy**:

* **RETAINED WITHOUT CHANGE (Verified Sound in Code & Contracts):**
* **Primary Pointwise Target & Non-Forgiveness (`instant_targets.py` Source 12, `instant_association.py` Source 17):** Retaining `verified_forward(b, H)` at 300s and 900s as the primary pointwise target and refusing to forgive pointwise failures via catalog association (`association_never_forgives_pointwise_failure: true`, Source 16) is a major methodological strength.


* **S1 Whole-Reference-Window Peak + Additive Forward-Peak Diagnostic (`instant_targets.py` Source 12, `instant_diagnostics.py` Source 13):** Retaining `pre_window_consumed_fraction` (with whole-window peak over $[b-30\text{s}, b+H]$) alongside `pre_window_to_forward_peak_consumed_fraction` and `whole_window_peak_precedes_anchor` in `instant_diagnostics.alert_report` (Source 13) completely resolves the S1/S3 reporting questions without altering the pinned `92fb51d` evaluator bytes.


* **S2 Causal Baseline Cutoffs (`micro_baseline.py` Source 14):** Retaining `sigma_end = floor((anchor - 35000)/60000)*60000` (60 closed 1m returns) and `pace_end = anchor - 30000` (120 non-overlapping 30s windows) is strictly causal and properly separates 1m volatility scaling from 30s activity baselines.


* **Earliest-Unused Greedy Forward-Window Coverage (`instant_association.py` Source 17):** Retaining deterministic chronological `[anchor - 180s, forward_peak]` matching and reporting `uncovered_forward_window_rate` (with `physical_missed_move_rate = None`) is transparent and prevents post-hoc matching optimization.


* **Own-Direction Gradability & Causal Exposure (`instant_directional_exposure.py` Source 19):** Retaining own-direction gradability (`opposite_direction_label_required: False`) and causal decision-universe exposure independent of future label readiness is the correct denominator architecture.


* **Continuity-Epoch Trade Reconciliation (`instant_coverage.py` Source 25, `instant_input_evidence.py` Source 27):** Retaining disjoint $\le 4$-proof / $\le 10,000\text{ms}$ ordering-race reconciliation with strict `(connection_id, continuity_epoch)` barriers is verified on the preserved hour and prevents cross-reset contamination.


* **Boxcar Endpoint F1–F5 Proposals (`INSTANT_STUDY_FREEZE_CANDIDATE_20261030.md` Section 3, Source 01):** The five proposed single-input formulas (with F4's explicit `compression * max(d*r_coin_30s/sigma_1m, 0)` directional interaction and F3's separation of OBI integral from `spread_bps`) are well-scaled, falsifiable, and avoid overfitting multiple decay half-lives.




* **QUALIFIED (Sound Implementation, Requires Explicit Operational Wiring in Stage 2):**
* **Cutoff $b$ vs. Closing-Heartbeat Proof Availability (`instant_input_evidence.py` Source 27 & `instant_publication.py` Source 21):** This is **not a defect in Stage 1** (which intentionally emits `study_eligible: False` and records exact `asof = cutoff` vs. `asof = later` diagnostics, Source 28).
