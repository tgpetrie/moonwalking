## 3. Five-feature candidate, fixed before outcome inspection

These are proposed formulas, not implemented features or empirically selected
weights. Keep the fixed 30s/180s windows; do not optimize a window grid on the

The following makes one concrete candidate available for implementation

| Feature | Proposed single fitted input | Required comparison |
|---|---|---|
| F1 acceleration | `d * (r_coin_30s - r_coin_prior150s/5) / sigma_1m` using adjacent nonoverlapping endpoint returns | Current velocity and market-relative movement; verify incremental value beyond their levels |
| F2 signed participation | `d * ((buy_usd-sell_usd)/(buy_usd+sell_usd)) * log1p(volume_pace)`; volume pace is current 30s match USD divided by the median of 120 completed, nonoverlapping 30s windows ending at `b-30s` | Unsigned pace/count and the full model without F2 |
| F3 sampled book pressure | `d/30s * integral((bid_size-ask_size)/(bid_size+ask_size), b-30s..b)` over last-observed BBO state | Spread-only control plus F2; spread stays separately recorded rather than hidden in a second F3 predictor |
| F4 active compression/expansion | `compression * max(d*r_coin_30s/sigma_1m,0)`, where `compression=1/(1+log(max(high)/min(low))/(sigma_1m*sqrt(15)))` over traded closed candles in the prior 15 completed minutes | Sigma/F1 and the same candidate without F4 |
| F5 market relative | `d * (r_coin_180s - beta*r_BTC_180s) / sigma_1m`, with synchronized endpoints and beta already available at the decision cutoff | Raw coin movement, beta-fallback/BTC-regime strata and coin returns under stated cost scenarios |

For F2, require >=5 unique matches and positive measured baselines/denominators;
do not insert epsilon/neutral scores to manufacture availability. The feature
uses volume pace; the secondary activity trigger's count-or-volume maximum
remains a different quantity. Record every excluded grid row and its reason.
Raw match windows are `[b-30s,b)`, and the 120 baseline windows end at `b-30s`.

For F3, use receipt-ordered observation times and require valid, <=10s-old

unavailable; do not remove that duration and renormalize the rest to 30s.
This is sampled L1 information, not a continuous full-book measurement.

For F4, require >=10 traded, closed, already-available candles in the prior

outage. The explicit directional expansion interaction keeps this one input

Unnormalized decayed sums at fixed 30s/180s half-lives remain separately logged

increment; do not rebuild past states with a newly estimated beta. In contrast,

Support means prespecified meaningful incremental value beyond the comparator,

  spread, quote observation time, last live-match receipt time, and
  `seconds_since_last_trade` / `seconds_since_last_quote` separately.
- `trade_count`, `quote_vol_usd`, `taker_buy_usd`, `taker_sell_usd`, all from
  unique live match executions. Unknown sides require an unknown-side amount
  and unavailable F2; do not force a two-way split.
- `price_source` (`mid`, `last_trade_fallback`, `unavailable`), fallback
  reason, observed/carried flags, quote validity, endpoint ages, feature
  coverage and whether any source or connection gap affects the window.

Within a continuously verified healthy interval, reconstruct a missing 5s
row with zero observed trades/volume and carry the prior quote state and its
**original** observation timestamp. Do not create a new trade, fresh quote
or observed OHLC path. Carried midpoint values describe last-known state.
Missing rows during downtime are unknown, not zero-volume observations.
Completeness verdicts can arrive later; retain the verdict known at `t0`
and any later audit verdict separately. Never rewrite a published feature.

For primary 30s/3m midpoint features, require valid positive non-crossed BBO
at each return endpoint, quote age <=10s at each endpoint, continuous feed
coverage across the window, and at least one unique live match in it. Record
the true endpoint ages. This replaces the loose proposed +/-10s reference
search. Midpoint-path features also require valid quote coverage throughout
their calculation. An `IGNITING` candidate additionally needs >=5 matches
in its 30s window; a volume multiple alone cannot let one print pass.
These fixed candidate gates may exclude thin coins; report that coverage.

If only a last trade is available, retain it with `last_trade_fallback` on
that row and propagate the flag to affected features. Exclude such rows
from the primary midpoint acceleration/decay cohort and report a separate
fallback cohort. Do not mix sources or treat a missing spread as zero.
Ticker-derived OBI averages are time averages of last-observed BBO state,
with stale durations marked unavailable; six endpoint snapshots do not
establish a continuously observed book. A future L2 feed would be a separate
versioned source change.

Late WS matches belong to their actual receipt bucket. Recovered REST trades
need `recovery_receipt_ts_ms` and exchange-time attribution in a separate
reconciliation record. They may repair an offline coverage audit, but must
not retroactively enter an earlier live bucket or inflate the recovery
instant's trading pace. Windows with unresolved earlier gaps stay ineligible
for complete-flow features at the original cutoff.

### Storage budget and retention

There are 17,280 possible buckets/product/day, or **7,032,960 dense rows/day**.
At an illustrative 80 bytes/row this is **562.6 MB/day** before index, WAL and

At an equally unverified **15% sparse occupancy**, the count is 1,054,944

A proposed 7-day rolling `live_microstructure.sqlite` is only the live cache.

## 4. Correct the smoothing math and keep feature hypotheses separate

Keep raw 30s and 3m percentage-return columns available for interpretation.
For smoothed candidate inputs use an **unnormalized decayed sum of log-return
increments**, per product, with fixed half-lives `h = 30s` and `h = 180s`:

```text
delta = 5 seconds; q_h = 2 ** (-delta / h)
x_i,j = log(mid_i,j / mid_i,j-1) - beta_i * log(mid_BTC,j / mid_BTC,j-1)
S_i,h,j = q_h * S_i,h,j-1 + x_i,j
decayed_move_sigma_i,h,j = S_i,h,j / sigma_i,1m(t0)
```

Use synchronized valid midpoint endpoints and beta known before the
increment; unavailable BTC/beta provenance makes the residual unavailable.
The denominator is that coin's causal one-minute baseline volatility, with
the declared floor. This is a scaled move, not a calibrated Gaussian z-score
or a claim of equal variance across half-lives. Any feature scaling for a
fit is learned on discovery only and frozen before validation.

There is **no leading `lambda = 1-q` multiplier and no unit-sum
normalization**. Those produce a weighted average increment rather than a
decayed cumulative move. For an isolated 0.02 log-return impulse, its weight
at age 30s with a 30s half-life is 0.500, at 31s it is 0.48858, and at 35s
it is 0.44545. The contribution is respectively 0.01000, 0.00977 and 0.00891
in log-return units. The 31s value describes continuous exponential decay;
closed-bucket observations still update every 5s.

Maintain the state independently per coin and half-life. Warm up for at
least ten slow half-lives (30m) plus the required baseline history. A true
no-trade bucket with valid observed price state can contribute a zero
increment; an unknown/stale interval cannot. Reset or mark state warming
after such a gap and satisfy warmup again. Never bridge a missing interval
with a newly received multi-minute return presented as a 5s impulse.

F1-F5 remain candidate mechanisms; their independence and usefulness are
unproven. The source/interpretation corrections apply as follows:

| Feature | Corrected dependency / interpretation |
|---|---|
| F1 acceleration | Midpoint returns over non-overlapping 30s and preceding 150s, normalized by per-coin baseline; flagged trade-price fallbacks are excluded from the primary cohort. |
| F2 signed participation | `TakerImb * log(1 + PaceRatio)`, gated on >=5 unique matches. Imbalance uses match-derived buy/sell USD; pace uses prior completed matched-volume windows. Historical 1m candle volume is not measured 30s taker flow. |
| F3 book pressure | Valid observed ticker BBO/sizes and spread, with observation ages and time weighting; no claim to full-book coverage. Optional perp data cannot substitute for missing required spot features. |
| F4 compression | Prior closed, already available candle history; require >=10 traded candles in the prior 15 completed minutes. Missing or late-fetched candles cannot become earlier features. |
| F5 market residual | Coin and BTC endpoints at the same causal cutoff, with beta provenance; a beta fallback remains visible. |

Freeze exact formulas, minimum baseline sample counts, missing-feature
policy and model inputs in the implementation contract. Do not automatically
add both decayed sums as extra fitted predictors beyond the declared five,
or choose between smooth and boxcar variants using test outcomes.

## 5. Extension, stage, score and reliability

Keep `score_0_100` (ordinal), `direction`, `stage`, `reliability`, input
windows and forward horizon as separate fields. Missing required evidence
produces an unavailable score and reason, not a fabricated neutral 50.
Feature coverage means measured feature coverage, not a count of connected
vendors; an optional missing perp feed does not automatically remove F1-F5.

For this prospective candidate retain the proposal's extension definition:

```text
E = abs(r_15m) / (sigma_1m * sqrt(15))
  + abs(r_60m) / (sigma_1m * sqrt(60))
```

This is a sum of two overlapping standardized lookbacks, not the number of
standard deviations of one independent return. Use causal midpoint prices
and declare missing extension history unavailable.

**`EXTENDED` is a hard exclusion from early-warning eligibility**, before
percentile ranking. For the candidate, `E > 3.5`, or an absolute daily move
`>10%` with measured trailing daily USD volume `<$1M`, sets `EXTENDED`. Missing
extension/liquidity data must not be treated as proof of eligibility. Stage
precedence is `EXTENDED`, then `FADING/ABSORBED`, then `CONFIRMED`, then
`IGNITING`; otherwise stage is unclassified. `E == 3.5` is not extended
by the E gate. Only complete-data `IGNITING` / `CONFIRMED` candidates enter
the early board, in separate UP/DOWN cohorts. Extended/fading rows may be
retained in shadow diagnostics or a separate risk-context view.

The original soft damper `exp(-max(0,E-3)^2/8)` retains 60.7% at E=5 and
32.5% at E=6 and cannot ensure exclusion. Dividing by 2 would retain 13.5%
and 1.1%; that remains a different hypothesis. The hard stage gate is the
chosen policy here. Do not infer the AURORA mockup's score of 18 from either
formula, or add another penalty/threshold search to this pilot.

Exact stage thresholds other than the extension/activity gates must be
frozen before fitting. Stage names do not establish "<30% of the move
elapsed"; that quantity requires future labels and cannot define live stage.

## 1. V2 Target Architecture & Resolution of V1 Limitations

### 1. Primary `verified_forward` Target vs. Secondary `verified_onset` Diagnostic

In `INSTANT_TARGET_CONTRACT_V2_DRAFT.json` (`targets.primary_pointwise: "verified_forward"`) and `TargetEvaluator.evaluate()` (`scripts/scoring_experiment/instant_targets.py`), every causally eligible 5s grid point across all products (including non-board and zero-move rows) receives a complete `verified_forward` evaluation without requiring the 30s velocity crossing (`velocity_crossing_sigma: 1.0`), 30s match pace (`> 2.0`), 180s breakout deadline, or `no_retrace` gate.

* **What This Resolves:** In V1, using `verified_onset` as the primary target coupled the label to 30s velocity and activity thresholds. Making `verified_forward` the primary pointwise target decouples the outcome label `(anchor, anchor + H]` from the pre-anchor 30s trigger window, while retaining `verified_onset` and `onset_candidate` strictly for secondary attrition and gate-conditioned continuation analysis.
* **Remaining Selection / Shared-Control Nuance:** As correctly noted in `INSTANT_TARGET_REVIEW_TRIAGE_20261030.md`, `verified_forward` still shares causal scale parameters (`sigma_1m`, `beta`, and entry `spread_bps` in `required_terminal_log`) with the candidate features. Evaluation must therefore stratify or control for baseline volatility, spread, and `beta_fallback` regimes rather than assuming `verified_forward` is statistically independent of feature scaling.

### 2. Separate `forward_event_catalog` and Explicit Absence of Physical Onset

`forward_event_catalog(labels)` (`instant_targets.py`) deduplicates `verified_forward == True` episodes over `[anchor, anchor + H)` per `(product_id, direction, horizon_seconds)` and stamps `"catalog_kind": "forward_window_not_physical_impulse"`.

* **What This Resolves:** As proven in `test_forward_catalog_retains_real_moves_with_small_retracement` (`tests/test_instant_targets_v2.py`) and `REPRODUCTIONS.json` (`one_bps_adverse_dip`), a 1 bp adverse dip at `ANCHOR + 5000` sets `verified_onset = False` and leaves `event_catalog([label])` empty, whereas `forward_event_catalog([label])` retains the episode.
* **Why Forward-Window Anchors Are Not Physical Onset (Hidden Timing Shift):** Neither a `forward_event_catalog` anchor nor `anchor - 30s` (`pre_window_reference_ts_ms`) represents the physical initiation of a move (`physical_onset_defined: False`). Furthermore, because `forward_event_catalog` selects the **earliest** 5s grid `anchor` whose `[anchor, anchor + H]` window satisfies `terminal >= threshold` and `retention >= 0.6`, a sharp 30-second impulse that starts at $t_{\text{phys}}$ will often qualify in `forward_event_catalog` at an earliest anchor $t_{\text{cat}} \approx t_{\text{phys}} - H + 30\text{s}$ (up to 4.5 minutes *before* the physical move on a 300s horizon, or 14.5 minutes early on a 900s horizon, provided price was flat during $[t_{\text{cat}}, t_{\text{phys}}]$). `forward_alert_timing()` properly avoids calling `publish_ts_ms < anchor` "pre-onset prediction," labeling it `"before_forward_window_anchor"` instead.

### 3. Named Pre-Window (`anchor - 30s`) and Post-Anchor Consumed Fractions

In `TargetEvaluator._v2_alert_timing()` (`instant_targets.py`), the unqualified `consumed_fraction` key is removed (`unqualified_consumed_fraction_allowed: false`) and replaced by two named metrics:

* `post_anchor_consumed_fraction`: `self._residual(anchor, ts, beta, sign) / peak_residual_log`
* `pre_window_consumed_fraction`: `self._residual(anchor - 30000, ts, beta, sign) / pre_window_peak_residual_log`, where `pre_window_peak_residual_log` is the maximum residual from `anchor - 30000` across `[anchor - 30000, anchor + H]`.
* **What This Resolves:** At `publish_ts_ms = ANCHOR` on the standard synthetic ramp (`REPRODUCTIONS.json`), V2 reports both `post_anchor_consumed_fraction = 0.0` and `pre_window_consumed_fraction = 0.002 / 0.022 = 0.090909...` (9.09%), while explicitly flagging `pre_window_reference_is_physical_origin: False`.

1. **S1 Decision (Peak Search Interval for `pre_window`):** Keep `pre_window_reference_ts_ms = anchor - 30_000` as the **price baseline** for the numerator `self._residual(anchor - 30_000, ts, beta, sign)`, but restrict the peak timestamp and denominator search to the **post-anchor forward horizon `[anchor, anchor + H]**` (so `pre_window_peak_ts_ms == peak_ts_ms`, and `pre_window_peak_residual_log = self._residual(anchor - 30_000, peak_ts_ms, beta, sign)`). 3. **S3 Decision (Explicit Reason for Null `pre_window_consumed_fraction`):** Add `"pre_window_consumed_fraction_reason"` to `_v2_alert_timing()` output, set to `label.get("pre_window_reference_reason")` when `pre_window_reference_status != "ready"`, `"nonpositive_pre_window_peak"` when `reference_peak <= 0` (as in `test_reference_may_have_no_positive_peak_for_an_independent_forward_move`), and `None` when populated.

## 3. F1–F5 Candidate Mechanism Review (Under V2 Target Architecture)

Per `INSTANT_TARGET_REVIEW_TRIAGE_20261030.md` and `INSTANT_TARGET_CONTRACT_V2_DRAFT.json`:

* **No Overstated Algebraic Guarantees:** As demonstrated in `test_crossing_does_not_algebraically_force_positive_F1_acceleration` (`fast = 0.002`, `prior / 5 = 0.0024`, acceleration `= -0.0004 < 0`) and `test_trade_count_pace_can_trigger_while_volume_pace_is_below_two` (`pace_multiple = 3` via trade count while USD volume pace is `60 / 100 = 0.6 < 2`), `onset_candidate` does not algebraically guarantee positive F1 or volume-pace $> 2$. Nevertheless, gate contamination still exists whenever predictors and triggers share inputs, which is why primary evaluation uses `verified_forward` and gate-conditioned continuation includes the gate inputs (`velocity_sigma`, `previous_velocity_sigma`, `pace_multiple`, `recent_trade_count`) in the null comparator.
* **Metric & Inference Discipline:** Any probability metric (such as log-loss or Brier score) requires a separately declared probabilistic calibration model; ordinal `score_0_100` rankings alone must be evaluated via rank/top-$K$ hit rates and matched-coverage precision, not uncalibrated log-loss. A paired UTC-day block bootstrap confidence interval spanning zero is **inconclusive** (insufficient power/regimes over 7 validation days), never proof of zero effect.

def load_contract(path=None):
    raw = Path(path or CONTRACT_PATH).read_text(encoding="utf-8").encode("utf-8")
    return json.loads(raw), hashlib.sha256(raw).hexdigest()


@dataclass(frozen=True)
class Sample:
    """State available at ts_ms; counts/volume cover [ts_ms-5000, ts_ms)."""

    product_id: str
    ts_ms: int
    bid: float | None
    ask: float | None
    btc_bid: float | None
    btc_ask: float | None
    quote_ts_ms: int
    btc_quote_ts_ms: int
    available_ts_ms: int
    trade_count: int
    quote_volume_usd: float
    coverage_verified: bool
    price_source: str = "mid"
    btc_price_source: str = "mid"
    trade_count_at_bucket_start: int | None = None

    @property
    def mid(self):
        return (self.bid + self.ask) / 2

    @property
    def btc_mid(self):
        return (self.btc_bid + self.btc_ask) / 2

    @property
    def spread_bps(self):
        return (self.ask - self.bid) / self.mid * 10000

def finite(value):
    return (
        isinstance(value, (float, int))
        and not isinstance(value, bool)
        and math.isfinite(value)
    )

    def __init__(self, samples, contract_path=None):

        if self.cfg["version"] not in ("instant-target-v1", "instant-target-v2-draft"):
            raise ValueError("unsupported target contract")
        self.is_v2 = self.cfg["version"] == "instant-target-v2-draft"
        self.implementation_sha256 = hashlib.sha256(
            Path(__file__).read_text(encoding="utf-8").encode("utf-8")
        ).hexdigest()
        self.rows = {}
        self.product_id = None
        previous = None
        for row in samples:
            if (
                not isinstance(row.ts_ms, int)
                or isinstance(row.ts_ms, bool)
                or row.ts_ms % self.cfg["grid_ms"]
            ):
                raise ValueError("sample timestamps must lie on the 5s grid")
            if previous is not None and row.ts_ms <= previous:
                raise ValueError("samples must be strictly increasing and unique")
            if self.product_id is not None and row.product_id != self.product_id:
                raise ValueError("one product per evaluator")
            self.product_id = row.product_id
            self.rows[row.ts_ms] = row
            previous = row.ts_ms
        if not self.rows:
            raise ValueError("samples are required")

    def _quality(self, start, end):
        for ts in range(start, end + 1, self.cfg["grid_ms"]):
            row = self.rows.get(ts)
            if row is None:
                return "missing_grid_point"
            if row.coverage_verified is not True:
                return "unverified_coverage"
            if any(
                type(value) is not int
                for value in (row.available_ts_ms, row.quote_ts_ms, row.btc_quote_ts_ms)
            ):
                return "invalid_timestamp"
            if row.available_ts_ms > ts:
                return "future_receipt"
            if row.price_source != "mid" or row.btc_price_source != "mid":
                return "price_fallback"
            for bid, ask, observed in (
                (row.bid, row.ask, row.quote_ts_ms),
                (row.btc_bid, row.btc_ask, row.btc_quote_ts_ms),
            ):
                if not finite(bid) or not finite(ask) or bid <= 0 or ask < bid:
                    return "invalid_quote"
                if not 0 <= ts - observed <= self.cfg["max_quote_age_ms"]:
                    return "stale_or_future_quote"
            if type(row.trade_count) is not int or row.trade_count < 0:
                return "invalid_trade_count"
            if not finite(row.quote_volume_usd) or row.quote_volume_usd < 0:
                return "invalid_volume"
        return None

    def _baseline_quality(self, baseline, anchor):
        if baseline is None:
            return "missing_baseline"
        if (
            any(
                type(value) is not int
                for value in (
                    baseline.return_count,
                    baseline.end_ts_ms,
                    baseline.available_ts_ms,
                    baseline.beta_available_ts_ms,
                )
            )
            or type(baseline.beta_fallback) is not bool
        ):
            return "invalid_baseline_metadata"
        if (
            not finite(baseline.sigma_1m)
            or baseline.sigma_1m < 0
            or not finite(baseline.beta)
        ):
            return "invalid_baseline"
        if baseline.return_count < self.cfg["minimum_baseline_returns"]:
            return "baseline_warming"
        baseline_cutoff = anchor
        if self.is_v2:
            # Reviewed from Claude d711832. Apply only in the explicit v2
            # draft so the pinned v1 definition and outputs remain reproducible.
            baseline_cutoff -= (
                self.cfg["onset"]["velocity_window_seconds"] * 1000
                + self.cfg["grid_ms"]
            )
        if baseline.end_ts_ms != baseline_cutoff // 60000 * 60000:
            return "baseline_window_misaligned"
        if baseline.available_ts_ms > anchor or baseline.beta_available_ts_ms > anchor:
            return "future_baseline"
        return None

    def _residual(self, start, end, beta, direction):
        a, b = self.rows[start], self.rows[end]
        return direction * (
            math.log(b.mid / a.mid) - beta * math.log(b.btc_mid / a.btc_mid)
        )

    def evaluate(self, anchor, baseline, direction, horizon_seconds, asof_ts_ms):
        """Grade every eligible grid anchor, including no-move observations.

        Status 'ready' means a complete label, not a successful impulse.
        All returned outcome/onset fields are evaluation-only.
        """
        if type(horizon_seconds) is not int or type(asof_ts_ms) is not int:
            raise ValueError("horizon and as-of timestamp must be integers")
        if (
            direction not in ("up", "down")
            or horizon_seconds not in self.cfg["outcome_horizons_seconds"]
        ):
            raise ValueError("unsupported direction or horizon")
        if type(anchor) is not int or anchor % self.cfg["grid_ms"]:
            raise ValueError("anchor must lie on the 5s grid")
        sign = 1 if direction == "up" else -1
        end = anchor + horizon_seconds * 1000
        result = dict(
            contract_version=self.cfg["version"],
            contract_sha256=self.contract_sha256,
            evaluation_only=True,
            product_id=self.product_id,
            anchor_ts_ms=anchor,
            horizon_seconds=horizon_seconds,
            direction=direction,
            label_maturity_ts_ms=end,
            status="pending",
            reason="horizon_not_mature",
            verified_forward=None,
            verified_onset=None,
        )
        if self.is_v2:
            result.update(
                evaluator_sha256=self.implementation_sha256,
                primary_pointwise_target="verified_forward",
                physical_onset_defined=False,
            )
        if asof_ts_ms < end:
            return result
        reason = self._baseline_quality(baseline, anchor) or self._quality(anchor, end)
        if reason:
            return {**result, "status": "unavailable", "reason": reason}
        boundary_count = self.rows[
            anchor + self.cfg["grid_ms"]
        ].trade_count_at_bucket_start
        if (
            type(boundary_count) is not int
            or not 0
            <= boundary_count
            <= self.rows[anchor + self.cfg["grid_ms"]].trade_count
        ):
            return {
                **result,
                "status": "unavailable",
                "reason": "unknown_boundary_trade_count",
            }
        sigma = max(baseline.sigma_1m, self.cfg["sigma_floor_1m"])
        path = [
            (ts, self._residual(anchor, ts, baseline.beta, sign))
            for ts in range(anchor, end + 1, self.cfg["grid_ms"])
        ]
        peak_ts, peak = max(path, key=lambda pair: pair[1])  # first tie wins
        terminal = path[-1][1]
        adverse = min(value for _, value in path)
        retention = terminal / peak if peak > 0 else None
        first, last = self.rows[anchor], self.rows[end]
        rules = self.cfg["impulse"]
        threshold = max(
            rules["terminal_sigma_multiple"] * sigma * math.sqrt(horizon_seconds / 60),
            rules["minimum_terminal_log_return"],
            rules["entry_spread_multiple"] * first.spread_bps / 10000,
        )
        trades = sum(self.rows[ts].trade_count for ts, _ in path[1:]) - boundary_count
        tolerance = self.cfg["comparison_tolerance"]
        verified = (
            terminal + tolerance >= threshold
            and retention is not None
            and retention + tolerance >= rules["minimum_peak_retention"]
            and trades >= rules["minimum_forward_matches"]
        )
        gross_bps = sign * (last.mid / first.mid - 1) * 10000
        spread_cost = (first.spread_bps + last.spread_bps) / 2
        result.update(
            status="ready",
            reason=None,
            verified_forward=verified,
            sigma_1m=sigma,
            sigma_floor_applied=baseline.sigma_1m < sigma,
            beta=baseline.beta,
            beta_fallback=baseline.beta_fallback,
            terminal_residual_log=terminal,
            required_terminal_log=threshold,
            peak_residual_log=peak,
            peak_ts_ms=peak_ts,
            adverse_residual_log=adverse,
            retention=retention,
            forward_trade_count=trades,
            gross_coin_return_bps=gross_bps,
            cost_scenarios=[
                dict(
                    fee_bps_per_side=fee,
                    net_coin_return_bps=gross_bps - spread_cost - 2 * fee,
                )
                for fee in self.cfg["costs"]["fee_bps_per_side_scenarios"]
            ],
        )
        onset = self.cfg["onset"]
        velocity_ms = onset["velocity_window_seconds"] * 1000
        step = self.cfg["grid_ms"]
        if self.is_v2:
            reference = anchor - velocity_ms
            context_reason = self._quality(reference, anchor)
            result.update(
                pre_window_reference_ts_ms=reference,
                pre_window_reference_is_physical_origin=False,
                pre_window_reference_status=(
                    "unavailable" if context_reason else "ready"
                ),
                pre_window_reference_reason=context_reason,
            )
            if not context_reason:
                reference_path = [
                    (ts, self._residual(reference, ts, baseline.beta, sign))
                    for ts in range(reference, end + 1, step)
                ]
                reference_peak_ts, reference_peak = max(
                    reference_path, key=lambda pair: pair[1]
                )
                result.update(
                    pre_window_peak_ts_ms=reference_peak_ts,
                    pre_window_peak_residual_log=reference_peak,
                )
        reason = self._quality(anchor - velocity_ms - step, anchor)
        if any(
            type(value) is not int
            for value in (
                baseline.pace_window_count,
                baseline.pace_end_ts_ms,
                baseline.pace_available_ts_ms,
            )
        ):
            return {
                **result,
                "onset_status": "unavailable",
                "onset_reason": "invalid_pace_metadata",
            }
        if baseline.pace_window_count < onset["minimum_prior_pace_windows"]:
            reason = reason or "pace_warming"
        if (
            baseline.pace_end_ts_ms != anchor - velocity_ms
            or baseline.pace_available_ts_ms > anchor
        ):
            reason = reason or "future_pace_baseline"
        medians = (baseline.pace_trade_median_30s, baseline.pace_quote_usd_median_30s)
        if not all(finite(value) and value >= 0 for value in medians) or not any(
            value > 0 for value in medians
        ):
            reason = reason or "pace_unavailable"
        if reason:
            return {**result, "onset_status": "unavailable", "onset_reason": reason}
        current_velocity = self._residual(
            anchor - velocity_ms, anchor, baseline.beta, sign
        ) / (sigma / math.sqrt(2))
        previous_velocity = self._residual(
            anchor - velocity_ms - step, anchor - step, baseline.beta, sign
        ) / (sigma / math.sqrt(2))
        recent = [
            self.rows[ts] for ts in range(anchor - velocity_ms + step, anchor + 1, step)
        ]
        recent_trades = sum(row.trade_count for row in recent)
        recent_volume = sum(row.quote_volume_usd for row in recent)
        pace = max(
            (
                value / median
                for value, median in zip((recent_trades, recent_volume), medians)
                if median > 0
            ),
            default=0,
        )
        candidate = (
            previous_velocity < onset["velocity_crossing_sigma"] - tolerance
            and current_velocity + tolerance >= onset["velocity_crossing_sigma"]
            and recent_trades >= onset["minimum_matches_30s"]
            and pace > onset["pace_multiple_strictly_greater_than"] + tolerance
        )
        breakout_ts = next(
            (
                ts
                for ts, value in path[1:]
                if ts <= anchor + onset["breakout_deadline_seconds"] * 1000
                and value + tolerance >= onset["breakout_sigma"] * sigma
            ),
            None,
        )
        no_retrace = adverse >= -onset["no_retrace_tolerance_log"]
        result.update(
            onset_status="ready",
            onset_reason=None,
            onset_candidate=candidate,
            previous_velocity_sigma=previous_velocity,
            velocity_sigma=current_velocity,
            pace_multiple=pace,
            recent_trade_count=recent_trades,
            breakout_ts_ms=breakout_ts,
            no_retrace=no_retrace,
            verified_onset=bool(
                candidate and verified and breakout_ts is not None and no_retrace
            ),
        )
        return result

    def alert_timing(self, label, publish_ts_ms):
        """Retrospective event association; never a live stage or input."""
        if self.is_v2:
            return self._v2_alert_timing(label, publish_ts_ms, strict_trigger=True)
        if type(publish_ts_ms) is not int:
            raise ValueError("publication timestamp must be an integer")
        if (
            label.get("contract_sha256") != self.contract_sha256
            or label.get("product_id") != self.product_id
            or not label.get("verified_onset")
        ):
            raise ValueError(
                "a verified onset from this contract and product is required"
            )
        anchor = label["anchor_ts_ms"]
        rules = self.cfg["events"]
        if (
            not anchor - rules["prediction_lead_limit_seconds"] * 1000
            <= publish_ts_ms
            <= label["label_maturity_ts_ms"]
        ):
            return dict(evaluation_only=True, timing="outside_event_window")
        # Backward as-of lookup only. Never use the next grid point at publication.
        ts = publish_ts_ms // self.cfg["grid_ms"] * self.cfg["grid_ms"]
        reason = self._quality(ts, ts)
        if reason:
            return dict(evaluation_only=True, timing="unavailable", reason=reason)
        row = self.rows[ts]
        if (
            max(publish_ts_ms - row.quote_ts_ms, publish_ts_ms - row.btc_quote_ts_ms)
            > self.cfg["max_quote_age_ms"]
        ):
            return dict(
                evaluation_only=True, timing="unavailable", reason="stale_alert_quote"
            )
        sign = 1 if label["direction"] == "up" else -1
        consumed = (
            self._residual(anchor, ts, label["beta"], sign) / label["peak_residual_log"]
        )
        timing = (
            "pre_onset_prediction"
            if publish_ts_ms < anchor
            else (
                "early_detection"
                if publish_ts_ms <= label["peak_ts_ms"]
                and consumed
                < rules["early_detection_consumed_fraction_strictly_below"]
                - self.cfg["comparison_tolerance"]
                else "late_detection"
            )
        )
        return dict(
            evaluation_only=True,
            timing=timing,
            detection_delay_seconds=(publish_ts_ms - anchor) / 1000,
            lead_to_peak_seconds=(label["peak_ts_ms"] - publish_ts_ms) / 1000,
            consumed_fraction=consumed,
            price_snapshot_ts_ms=ts,
        )

    def forward_alert_timing(self, label, publish_ts_ms):
        """v2 forward-window association, without inferring a physical onset."""
        if not self.is_v2:
            raise ValueError("forward-window timing requires the explicit v2 draft")
        return self._v2_alert_timing(label, publish_ts_ms, strict_trigger=False)

    def _v2_alert_timing(self, label, publish_ts_ms, strict_trigger):
        if type(publish_ts_ms) is not int:
            raise ValueError("publication timestamp must be an integer")
        required = "verified_onset" if strict_trigger else "verified_forward"
        if (
            label.get("contract_sha256") != self.contract_sha256
            or label.get("evaluator_sha256") != self.implementation_sha256
            or label.get("product_id") != self.product_id
            or label.get("status") != "ready"
            or label.get(required) is not True
        ):
            raise ValueError(
                "a complete verified label from this evaluator is required"
            )
        peak = label.get("peak_residual_log")
        if not finite(peak) or peak <= 0:
            raise ValueError("a positive finite forward peak is required")
        anchor = label["anchor_ts_ms"]
        lead_ms = self.cfg["events"]["prediction_lead_limit_seconds"] * 1000
        if not anchor - lead_ms <= publish_ts_ms <= label["label_maturity_ts_ms"]:
            return dict(evaluation_only=True, timing="outside_event_window")
        ts = publish_ts_ms // self.cfg["grid_ms"] * self.cfg["grid_ms"]
        reason = self._quality(ts, ts)
        if reason:
            return dict(evaluation_only=True, timing="unavailable", reason=reason)
        row = self.rows[ts]
        if (
            max(publish_ts_ms - row.quote_ts_ms, publish_ts_ms - row.btc_quote_ts_ms)
            > self.cfg["max_quote_age_ms"]
        ):
            return dict(
                evaluation_only=True, timing="unavailable", reason="stale_alert_quote"
            )
        sign = 1 if label["direction"] == "up" else -1
        post_fraction = self._residual(anchor, ts, label["beta"], sign) / peak
        output = dict(
            evaluation_only=True,
            physical_onset_defined=False,
            post_anchor_consumed_fraction=post_fraction,
            post_anchor_reference_ts_ms=anchor,
            offset_from_anchor_seconds=(publish_ts_ms - anchor) / 1000,
            lead_to_forward_peak_seconds=(label["peak_ts_ms"] - publish_ts_ms) / 1000,
            price_snapshot_ts_ms=ts,
            pre_window_consumed_fraction=None,
            pre_window_reference_ts_ms=label.get("pre_window_reference_ts_ms"),
            pre_window_reference_is_physical_origin=False,
        )
        reference_peak = label.get("pre_window_peak_residual_log")
        if (
            label.get("pre_window_reference_status") == "ready"
            and finite(reference_peak)
            and reference_peak > 0
        ):
            output["pre_window_consumed_fraction"] = (
                self._residual(
                    label["pre_window_reference_ts_ms"], ts, label["beta"], sign
                )
                / reference_peak
            )
            output["lead_to_pre_window_peak_seconds"] = (
                label["pre_window_peak_ts_ms"] - publish_ts_ms
            ) / 1000
        if strict_trigger:
            fraction = output["pre_window_consumed_fraction"]
            if fraction is None:
                return dict(
                    evaluation_only=True,
                    timing="unavailable",
                    reason="pre_window_reference_unavailable",
                )
            early = (
                publish_ts_ms <= label["pre_window_peak_ts_ms"]
                and fraction
                < self.cfg["events"]["early_detection_consumed_fraction_strictly_below"]
                - self.cfg["comparison_tolerance"]
            )
            output["timing"] = (
                "before_activity_trigger"
                if publish_ts_ms < anchor
                else (
                    "early_after_activity_trigger"
                    if early
                    else "late_after_activity_trigger"
                )
            )
            output["association_kind"] = "strict_activity_trigger"
        else:
            output["timing"] = (
                "before_forward_window_anchor"
                if publish_ts_ms < anchor
                else (
                    "within_forward_window_before_peak"
                    if publish_ts_ms <= label["peak_ts_ms"]
                    else "within_forward_window_after_peak"
                )
            )
            output["association_kind"] = "forward_window_not_physical_impulse"
        return output


def event_catalog(labels, *, target="verified_onset"):
    """Earliest verified event owns [onset,onset+H), per product/direction/H.

    This retrospective deduplication is separate from the live 15m per-coin
    alert cooldown. Unverified/pending rows never suppress another event.
    """
    labels = list(labels)
    if target not in ("verified_onset", "verified_forward"):
        raise ValueError("unsupported catalog target")
    selected, ends = [], {}
    hashes = {row["contract_sha256"] for row in labels}
    if len(hashes) > 1:
        raise ValueError("cannot mix target contracts")
    implementations = {row.get("evaluator_sha256") for row in labels}
    if len(implementations) > 1:
        raise ValueError("cannot mix evaluator implementations")
    for row in sorted(
        labels,
        key=lambda row: (
            row["anchor_ts_ms"],
            row["product_id"],
            row["direction"],
            row["horizon_seconds"],
        ),
    ):
        key = row["product_id"], row["direction"], row["horizon_seconds"]
        if (
            row.get(target) is True
            and row.get("status") == "ready"
            and row["anchor_ts_ms"] >= ends.get(key, -math.inf)
        ):
            selected.append(row)
            ends[key] = row["label_maturity_ts_ms"]
    return selected


def forward_event_catalog(labels):
    """Catalog forward windows independently of activity/no-retrace gates.

    These are deduplicated qualifying forecast windows, not a definition of
    physical move onset or a completed operational missed-move denominator.
    """
    labels = list(labels)
    if any(row.get("contract_version") != "instant-target-v2-draft" for row in labels):
        raise ValueError(
            "forward-window catalog requires labels from the explicit v2 draft"
        )
    return [
        {**row, "catalog_kind": "forward_window_not_physical_impulse"}
        for row in event_catalog(labels, target="verified_forward")
    ]

def _integer(value):
    if type(value) is not int or value < 0:
        raise ValueError("nonnegative integer required")
    return value


def _grid(value):
    if _integer(value) % GRID_MS:
        raise ValueError("timestamps must lie on the 5s grid")
    return value


def _text(value):
    if not isinstance(value, str) or not value:
        raise ValueError("nonempty string identity required")
    return value


def _hours(slot_count):
    return slot_count * GRID_MS / 3_600_000


def provenance():

def _labels(labels, asof):
    """Index complete v2 labels by identity; reject mixed or malformed rows."""
    rows, contracts, evaluators = {}, set(), set()
    for row in labels:
        if not isinstance(row, dict) or any(k not in row for k in LABEL_KEYS):
            raise ValueError("malformed label row")
        if row["contract_version"] != TARGET_VERSION:
            raise ValueError("labels must come from the explicit v2 target draft")
        product, direction, horizon = (
            _text(row["product_id"]),
            row["direction"],
            row["horizon_seconds"],
        )
        if (
            direction not in DIRECTIONS
            or type(horizon) is not int
            or horizon not in HORIZONS
        ):
            raise ValueError("unsupported label direction or horizon")
        anchor, maturity = _grid(row["anchor_ts_ms"]), _integer(
            row["label_maturity_ts_ms"]
        )
        if maturity != anchor + horizon * 1000:
            raise ValueError("label maturity must equal anchor plus horizon")
        status, forward = row["status"], row["verified_forward"]
        if status == "ready":
            if type(forward) is not bool:
                raise ValueError("a ready label needs a boolean outcome; never coerce")
            if maturity > asof:
                raise ValueError("a ready label cannot mature after the report as-of")
            peak = row.get("peak_ts_ms")
            if type(peak) is not int or not anchor <= peak <= maturity:
                raise ValueError("a ready label needs a forward peak inside its window")
        elif status in ("pending", "unavailable"):
            if forward is not None:
                raise ValueError("an incomplete label cannot carry an outcome")
        else:
            raise ValueError("unknown label status")
        contracts.add(_text(row["contract_sha256"]))
        evaluators.add(_text(row["evaluator_sha256"]))
        key = (product, direction, horizon, anchor)
        if key in rows:
            raise ValueError("duplicate label identity")
        rows[key] = row
    if len(contracts) > 1 or len(evaluators) > 1:
        raise ValueError("cannot mix target contract or evaluator provenance")
    return rows, next(iter(contracts), None), next(iter(evaluators), None)


def _alerts(alerts, asof):
    """Retain anchor, input availability and actual publication separately."""
    out = {}
    for row in alerts:
        if not isinstance(row, dict) or any(k not in row for k in ALERT_KEYS):
            raise ValueError("malformed alert row")
        pid = _text(row["publication_id"])
        if pid in out:
            raise ValueError("duplicate publication identity")
        if row["direction"] not in DIRECTIONS:
            raise ValueError("unsupported alert direction")
        anchor, inputs, published = (
            _grid(row["anchor_ts_ms"]),
            _integer(row["inputs_available_ts_ms"]),
            _integer(row["published_ts_ms"]),
        )
        if not anchor <= inputs <= published <= asof:
            raise ValueError(
                "alert clocks must satisfy anchor <= inputs available <= published"
                " <= as-of; no late feature is known at an earlier anchor"
            )
        out[pid] = dict(
            publication_id=pid,
            product_id=_text(row["product_id"]),
            direction=row["direction"],
            anchor_ts_ms=anchor,
            inputs_available_ts_ms=inputs,
            published_ts_ms=published,
            input_lag_ms=inputs - anchor,
            publication_delay_ms=published - anchor,
            inputs_known_at_anchor=inputs == anchor,
        )
    return sorted(
        out.values(), key=lambda a: (a["published_ts_ms"], a["publication_id"])
    )


def _primary(label):
    if label is None:
        return "ungradable", "label_missing"
    if label["status"] == "ready":
        return ("success" if label["verified_forward"] else "failure"), None
    return "ungradable", f"{label['status']}:{label.get('reason') or 'no_reason'}"


def _ratio(numerator, denominator):
    return numerator / denominator if denominator else None


def association_report(labels, alerts, *, asof_ts_ms):

    _integer(asof_ts_ms)
    rows, contract_sha256, evaluator_sha256 = _labels(labels, asof_ts_ms)
    pubs = _alerts(alerts, asof_ts_ms)
    windows = {}
    for window in diag.forward_catalog_report(list(rows.values())):
        key = window["product_id"], window["direction"], window["horizon_seconds"]
        windows.setdefault(key, []).append(window)
    lead_ms = LEAD_LIMIT_SECONDS * 1000
    tables, forgiven = {}, 0
    for horizon in HORIZONS:
        outcomes, counts, reasons = [], Counter(), Counter()
        for alert in pubs:
            label = rows.get(
                (
                    alert["product_id"],
                    alert["direction"],
                    horizon,
                    alert["anchor_ts_ms"],
                )
            )
            outcome, reason = _primary(label)
            counts[outcome] += 1
            if reason:
                reasons[reason] += 1
            outcomes.append(
                {
                    **alert,
                    "horizon_seconds": horizon,
                    "primary_outcome": outcome,
                    "ungradable_reason": reason,
                    "label_status": label["status"] if label else None,
                    "verified_forward": label["verified_forward"] if label else None,
                    "covers_forward_window_anchor_ts_ms": None,
                }
            )
        covered, uncovered, used = [], [], {}
        for (product, direction, h), group in sorted(windows.items()):
            if h != horizon:
                continue
            candidates = [
                a
                for a in pubs
                if a["product_id"] == product and a["direction"] == direction
            ]
            for window in sorted(group, key=lambda w: w["anchor_ts_ms"]):
                start, peak = window["anchor_ts_ms"] - lead_ms, window["peak_ts_ms"]
                chosen = next(

                        a
                        for a in candidates
                        if a["publication_id"] not in used
                        and start <= a["published_ts_ms"] <= peak
                    ),
                    None,
                )
                record = dict(
                    product_id=product,
                    direction=direction,
                    horizon_seconds=horizon,

        unmatched = [
            a["publication_id"] for a in pubs if a["publication_id"] not in used
        ]
        window_count = len(covered) + len(uncovered)
        success, failure = counts["success"], counts["failure"]
        tables[str(horizon)] = dict(
            horizon_seconds=horizon,

                alert_count=len(pubs),
                success_count=success,

def exposure_report(labels, eligibility, *, asof_ts_ms, ranker_slots=None):
    """Causal decision-eligible exposure, separately from retrospective gradability."""
    _integer(asof_ts_ms)
    rows, contract_sha256, evaluator_sha256 = _labels(labels, asof_ts_ms)
    slots = {}
    for record in eligibility:
        if (
            not isinstance(record, dict)
            or "product_id" not in record
            or "ts_ms" not in record
            or any(type(record.get(flag)) is not bool for flag in ELIGIBILITY_FLAGS)
        ):
            raise ValueError(
                "explicit boolean eligibility flags required; never coerce"
            )
        key = _text(record["product_id"]), _grid(record["ts_ms"])
        if key[1] > asof_ts_ms:
            raise ValueError("eligibility after the report as-of is not known")
        if key in slots:
            raise ValueError("duplicate or overlapping eligibility slot")
        slots[key] = {flag: record[flag] for flag in ELIGIBILITY_FLAGS}
    universe = Counter(ts for (_, ts), f in slots.items() if f["universe_member"])
    cutoffs = sorted({ts for _, ts in slots})
    sizes = [[ts, universe.get(ts, 0)] for ts in cutoffs]
    eligible, exclusions = set(), Counter()
    for key, flags in slots.items():
        failed = next((flag for flag in ELIGIBILITY_FLAGS if not flags[flag]), None)
        if failed is None:
            eligible.add(key)
        else:
            exclusions[failed] += 1
    rankers = {}
    for name, scored in (ranker_slots or {}).items():
        records = [(_text(p), _grid(ts)) for p, ts in scored]
        scored = set(records)
        if len(scored) != len(records):
            raise ValueError("duplicate ranker slot")
        if not scored <= set(slots):
            raise ValueError("ranker slots must be recorded eligibility slots")
        rankers[_text(name)] = scored
    horizons = {}
    for horizon in HORIZONS:
        gradable, reasons = set(), Counter()
        for product, ts in sorted(eligible):
            reason = None
            if ts + horizon * 1000 > asof_ts_ms:
                reason = "immature_trailing_horizon"
            for direction in DIRECTIONS if reason is None else ():
                label = rows.get((product, direction, horizon, ts))
                if label is None:
                    reason = f"label_missing:{direction}"
                elif label["status"] != "ready":
                    reason = f"{label['status']}:{direction}:{label.get('reason')}"
                if reason:
                    break
            if reason:
                reasons[reason] += 1
            else:
                gradable.add((product, ts))
        shared = set.intersection(*rankers.values()) if rankers else set(eligible)
        horizons[str(horizon)] = dict(
            horizon_seconds=horizon,
            gradable_slot_count=len(gradable),
            gradable_product_hours=_hours(len(gradable)),
            ungradable_eligible_slot_count=len(eligible) - len(gradable),
            ungradable_reasons=dict(sorted(reasons.items())),
            gradable_fraction_of_eligible=_ratio(len(gradable), len(eligible)),
            comparison=dict(
                identical_complete_row_count=len(shared & gradable),
                identical_complete_rows_definition=(
                    "decision_eligible_and_gradable_and_scored_by_every_ranker"
                ),
                natural_ranker_coverage={
                    name: dict(
                        eligible_scored_slot_count=len(scored & eligible),
                        gradable_scored_slot_count=len(scored & gradable),
                    )
                    for name, scored in sorted(rankers.items())
                },
            ),
        )
    return dict(
        **provenance(),
        knowledge_asof_ts_ms=asof_ts_ms,
        target_contract_sha256=contract_sha256,
        target_evaluator_sha256=evaluator_sha256,
        recorded_slot_count=len(slots),
        decision_eligible_slot_count=len(eligible),
        decision_eligible_product_hours=_hours(len(eligible)),
        board_wall_clock_hours_with_any_eligible_product=_hours(
            len({ts for _, ts in eligible})

def _decimal(value, *, positive):
    if isinstance(value, bool):
        raise ValueError("boolean is not a book amount")
    try:
        result = Decimal(str(value))
        number = float(result)
    except (InvalidOperation, ValueError, OverflowError) as exc:
        raise ValueError("invalid book amount") from exc
    if (
        not result.is_finite()
        or not math.isfinite(number)
        or result < 0
        or (positive and (result <= 0 or number <= 0))
    ):
        raise ValueError("invalid book amount")
    if result > 0 and number <= 0:
        raise ValueError("book amount underflows reader representation")
    return result

class BookReplay:
    """Last fully processed book state, with original receipt age and known-at time.

    Quote state is not complete feed certification. The existing receipt/ID
    monitor retains its own narrower scope. No state here enters Sample/V1/V2.
    An injectable processing clock is a trusted test input, not historical proof.
    """

    def __init__(self, products, *, clock_ms=None):
        raw = CONTRACT.read_text(encoding="utf-8").encode("utf-8")
        cfg = json.loads(raw)
        if (cfg["version"], cfg["max_levels_per_product"], cfg["max_quote_age_ms"]) != (
            VERSION,
            MAX_LEVELS,
            MAX_QUOTE_AGE_MS,
        ):
            raise ValueError("book contract and implementation differ")
        self.monitor = CoverageMonitor(
            products, quote_channel="level2_batch", clock_ms=clock_ms
        )
        self.contract_sha256 = hashlib.sha256(raw).hexdigest()
        self.source_sha256 = hashlib.sha256(
            Path(__file__).read_text(encoding="utf-8").encode("utf-8")
        ).hexdigest()
        self.connection = None
        self.books = {}
        self.last_heartbeat = {}
        self.history = {p: [] for p in self.monitor.products}
        self.awaiting_ack = set(self.monitor.products)
        self.completed = {}  # receipt_seq -> this packet's book completion.
        self.failed = False

    def _invalidate(self, product, reason):
        self.books.pop(product, None)
        self.pending.append((product, reason))

    @staticmethod
    def _best(book):
        bid, ask = max(book["buy"]), min(book["sell"])
        if bid >= ask:
            raise ValueError("locked or crossed book")
        return tuple(map(str, (bid, ask, book["buy"][bid], book["sell"][ask])))

    def _snapshot(self, message):
        book = {"buy": {}, "sell": {}}
        for side, key in (("buy", "bids"), ("sell", "asks")):
            levels = message.get(key)
            if not isinstance(levels, list) or not levels or len(levels) > MAX_LEVELS:
                raise ValueError("bounded nonempty book side required")
            for row in levels:
                if not isinstance(row, list) or len(row) != 2:
                    raise ValueError("snapshot level must be [price,size]")
                price, size = (_decimal(v, positive=True) for v in row)
                if price in book[side]:
                    raise ValueError("duplicate snapshot price")
                book[side][price] = size
        return book

    def _update(self, previous, message):
        changes = message.get("changes")
        if not isinstance(changes, list) or not changes or len(changes) > MAX_LEVELS:
            raise ValueError("bounded nonempty change batch required")
        book = {side: previous[side].copy() for side in ("buy", "sell")}
        for row in changes:
            if not isinstance(row, list) or len(row) != 3 or row[0] not in book:
                raise ValueError("update must be [buy_or_sell,price,absolute_size]")
            side, price, size = (
                row[0],
                _decimal(row[1], positive=True),
                _decimal(row[2], positive=False),
            )
            if size == 0:
                book[side].pop(price, None)
            else:
                book[side][price] = size
        if not book["buy"] or not book["sell"]:
            raise ValueError("book side exhausted")
        return book

    def ingest(self, envelope):
        if self.failed:
            raise ValueError("book replay failed; no more input accepted")
        try:
            self.monitor.ingest(envelope)
            self.pending = []
            self._consume(envelope)
            finished = _integer(self.monitor.clock_ms())
            if finished < self.monitor.last[3]:
                raise ValueError("book processing clock regressed")
            self.monitor.last = (*self.monitor.last[:3], finished)
            receipt, seq = envelope["receipt_ts_ms"], envelope["receipt_seq"]
            # Monitor records from this packet are not book knowledge until the
            # book has processed the same packet (e.g. a heartbeat gap).
            self.completed[seq] = max(finished, receipt + 1)
            for product, reason in self.pending:
                book = self.books.get(product)
                self.history[product].append(
                    BookState(
                        product,
                        receipt,
                        max(finished, receipt + 1),
                        seq,
                        self.connection,

def _integer(value):
    if type(value) is not int or value < 0:
        raise ValueError("nonnegative integer required")
    return value


def _hash(path):
    result = hashlib.sha256()
    with Path(path).open("rb") as stream:
        for block in iter(lambda: stream.read(1024 * 1024), b""):
            result.update(block)
    return result.hexdigest()


def _amount(value):
    if isinstance(value, bool):
        raise ValueError("invalid match amount")
    try:
        result = Decimal(str(value))
    except InvalidOperation as exc:
        raise ValueError("invalid match amount") from exc
    if not result.is_finite() or result <= 0:
        raise ValueError("invalid match amount")
    return result


class CoverageMonitor:

    def _barrier(self, products):

    def ingest(self, envelope):

    def _reconcile(self, product, connection, epoch):
        """Re-check the trailing run of adjacent failed proofs as one span.

        Only a heartbeat/match ordering race is repairable: the merged span must
        satisfy the same exact rule (expected IDs == unique IDs received on this
        connection), so a truly lost ID still fails. Trade receipt times and
        bucket counts are untouched. ingest stamps the merged proof with the
        closing heartbeat's processing completion, so nothing is backdated.
        """
        run = []
        for proof in reversed(self.proofs):
            if proof["product_id"] != product:
                continue
            # Verified intervals and earlier merged proofs have no reason, so
            # they end the run: merges stay disjoint and a nested race fails closed.
            if (
                len(run) == MAX_MERGED_PROOFS
                or proof["reason"] not in MERGEABLE_REASONS
                or proof["connection_id"] != connection
                or proof["continuity_epoch"] != epoch
                or (
                    run
                    and (
                        proof["end_ts_ms"] != run[-1]["start_ts_ms"]
                        or proof["upper_trade_id_inclusive"]
                        != run[-1]["lower_trade_id_exclusive"]
                    )
                )
            ):
                break
            run.append(proof)
        # Smallest closing run first, so two separate races stay separate proofs.
        for size in range(2, len(run) + 1):
            first, last = run[size - 1], run[0]
            start, end = first["start_ts_ms"], last["end_ts_ms"]
            low = first["lower_trade_id_exclusive"]
            high = last["upper_trade_id_inclusive"]
            if end - start > MAX_MERGED_SPAN_MS or high - low > MAX_ID_SPAN:
                return
            left, right = (bisect_left(self.times[product], t) for t in (start, end))
            ids = {
                tid
                for _, tid, owner, era in self.trades[product][left:right]
                if owner == connection and era == epoch
            }
            if ids != set(range(low + 1, high + 1)):
                continue
            self.proofs.append(
                dict(
                    product_id=product,
                    start_ts_ms=start,
                    end_ts_ms=end,
                    available_ts_ms=None,
                    lower_trade_id_exclusive=low,
                    upper_trade_id_inclusive=high,
                    observed_unique_trade_count=len(ids),
                    expected_trade_count=high - low,
                    verified=True,
                    reason=None,
                    connection_id=connection,
                    continuity_epoch=epoch,
                    start_heartbeat_seq=first["start_heartbeat_seq"],
                    end_heartbeat_seq=last["end_heartbeat_seq"],
                    scope=MERGED_SCOPE,
                    merged_proof_count=size,
                    supersedes_end_heartbeat_seqs=sorted(
                        p["end_heartbeat_seq"] for p in run[:size]
                    ),
                )
            )
            return

    def assess(self, product, start, end, *, asof):
        if self.failed:
            raise ValueError("failed monitor cannot produce assessments")
        for value in (start, end, asof):
            _integer(value)
        if product not in self.trades or start >= end or asof < end:
            raise ValueError(
                "known product, completed interval and asof >= end required"
            )

            s
            for s in self.transport
            if s["product_id"] == product and s["available_ts_ms"] <= asof
        ]

        transport_reason = (
            state["reason"] if state else "no_known_subscription_or_heartbeat"
        )
        if state and state["reason"] == "heartbeat_observed":
            transport_reason = (
                None
                if asof - state["receipt_ts_ms"] <= MAX_HEARTBEAT_GAP_MS
                else "stale_heartbeat"

        spans = [
            p
            for p in self.proofs

            and p["end_ts_ms"] > start

            for seq in p["supersedes_end_heartbeat_seqs"]

            c["product_id"] == product

            and c["end_ts_ms"] > start
            and c["available_ts_ms"] <= asof
            for c in self.conflicts
        )

            "conflicting_trade_id"
            if conflict
            else next((p["reason"] for p in spans if not p["verified"]), None)
        )
        cursor, available, prior, merged_used = start, end, None, 0
        if reason is None:
            for span in sorted(spans, key=lambda p: (p["start_ts_ms"], p["end_ts_ms"])):

def _file_sha(path):
    digest = hashlib.sha256()

## Inventory accounting

The intent is the static cross product of declared products, every 5s cutoff
in `[first_cutoff, stop_cutoff)` and both directions. BTC is required as the
benchmark. Every expected row is retained, including products with no packet,
missing quotes or no proven trade interval. Empty observations do not shrink
the declared inventory or become healthy zero counts.

The canonical intent and its hash are separate from the time the recorder
actually loaded it. A file hash does not prove it was declared in advance.
`inventory_precedes_first_cutoff` requires the measured loading time to be
strictly earlier. Loading an old plan during replay records today's time and
does not claim prospective declaration. This validates completeness relative
to the submitted plan; it cannot independently prove that the plan contains
the intended exchange universe. That universe still needs an external,
predeclared identity/source review.

Limits are 10,000 inventory rows, 32 products, the existing 100,000-packet
monitor bound and existing book-level bounds. These are offline guards, not
a measured one-hour capacity profile or new capture permission. A tape must
start at local receipt sequence 1 and remain contiguous. Bounds, malformed
packets or clock regression fail the artifact closed. Sealing requires all
planned cutoff times to have been reached; no prefix is certified a complete capture.
An empty tape can yield complete **inventory accounting** with all rows
unavailable; it is never called a complete or healthy capture.

## Processing and component evidence

Packet evidence stores receipt sequence/time, measured processing start and
completion, availability at least receipt plus 1ms, and canonical envelope
hash. Unique live-match rows retain their first receipt, packet completion,
connection and continuity epoch, exact Decimal price/size and inverted maker
side. Unknown sides remain separate. REST recovery, ticker sizes and
`last_match` do not create execution rows; duplicates never move first receipt.
Completion measures book/trade payload processing, not disk commit or durable
publication. Injected clocks remain trusted synthetic-test inputs.

Coin and BTC evidence are computed separately:

- The quote component uses the existing last processed book view and original
  observation age. Its selected state and heartbeat must also have completed
  this producer's processing by the requested knowledge time. A heartbeat
  cannot refresh quote age; an unfinished quote cannot borrow book-only
  completion. No continuous 30s F3 path is certified.
- The 30s trade component uses exact receipt-ID proofs plus completed trade
  rows, scoped to the verifying connection and continuity epoch. A first
  receipt at a shared-millisecond reset stays in its original epoch and cannot
  be certified by the following epoch. Known raw counts remain diagnostic;
  verified counts/amounts stay null without these checks. Only a proven empty
  receipt window becomes zero. Unknown taker side prevents signed-flow use.

`checkpoint(cutoff)` freezes the full product/direction cohort with component
knowledge at that cutoff and a separately measured assessment-completion
time. Returned rows are frozen and their nested component payload is serialized;
decoded copies cannot mutate them. Later packets, conflicts or failures never
rewrite the frozen cohort. Local logger/clock corruption still fails the whole
artifact closed, rather than exporting earlier rows from a failed run.

`component_evidence(product, cutoff, asof=later)` is a separately marked later
diagnostic. For a window ending at 30,000ms, its closing heartbeat at 30,000ms
is not available before 30,001ms even with no processing delay. The later
diagnostic can then report a proven zero; the frozen 30,000ms row remains
unproven. Neither result is full-feed or quote-continuity certification.
