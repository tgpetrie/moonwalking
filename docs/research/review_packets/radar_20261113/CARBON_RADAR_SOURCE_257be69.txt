## 3. Five-second storage, reconstruction and freshness

**Choose sparse persistence and reconstruct logical empty buckets on read.**

Use receipt-time buckets `[start, start+5000)`. Alongside the sparse table,

can be distinguished from collector downtime. No reconstruction across an
unverified interval is permitted.

Minimum row/provenance fields:

- Canonical `product_id`, `bucket_start_ts_ms`, first/last receipt times,
  last exchange event time per source, connection/receipt ordering fields,
  and first/last match trade IDs with coverage status.
- Observed midpoint OHLC, last trade price, last observed bid/ask and sizes,

- `trade_count`, `quote_vol_usd`, `taker_buy_usd`, `taker_sell_usd`, all from

Within a continuously verified healthy interval, reconstruct a missing 5s

## 4. Correct the smoothing math and keep feature hypotheses separate

Keep raw 30s and 3m percentage-return columns available for interpretation.
For smoothed candidate inputs use an **unnormalized decayed sum of log-return
increments**, per product, with fixed half-lives `h = 30s` and `h = 180s`:

```text
delta = 5 seconds; q_h = 2 ** (-delta / h)
x_i,j = log(mid_i,j / mid_i,j-1) - beta_i * log(mid_BTC,j / mid_BTC,j-1)
S_i,h,j = q_h * S_i,h,j-1 + x_i,j
decayed_move_sigma_i,h,j = S_i,h,j / sigma_i,1m(t0)
```

Use synchronized valid midpoint endpoints and beta known before the
increment; unavailable BTC/beta provenance makes the residual unavailable.
The denominator is that coin's causal one-minute baseline volatility, with
the declared floor. This is a scaled move, not a calibrated Gaussian z-score
or a claim of equal variance across half-lives. Any feature scaling for a
fit is learned on discovery only and frozen before validation.

There is **no leading `lambda = 1-q` multiplier and no unit-sum
normalization**. Those produce a weighted average increment rather than a
decayed cumulative move. For an isolated 0.02 log-return impulse, its weight
at age 30s with a 30s half-life is 0.500, at 31s it is 0.48858, and at 35s
it is 0.44545. The contribution is respectively 0.01000, 0.00977 and 0.00891
in log-return units. The 31s value describes continuous exponential decay;
closed-bucket observations still update every 5s.

Maintain the state independently per coin and half-life. Warm up for at
least ten slow half-lives (30m) plus the required baseline history. A true
no-trade bucket with valid observed price state can contribute a zero
increment; an unknown/stale interval cannot. Reset or mark state warming
after such a gap and satisfy warmup again. Never bridge a missing interval
with a newly received multi-minute return presented as a 5s impulse.

F1-F5 remain candidate mechanisms; their independence and usefulness are
unproven. The source/interpretation corrections apply as follows:

| Feature | Corrected dependency / interpretation |
|---|---|
| F1 acceleration | Midpoint returns over non-overlapping 30s and preceding 150s, normalized by per-coin baseline; flagged trade-price fallbacks are excluded from the primary cohort. |
| F2 signed participation | `TakerImb * log(1 + PaceRatio)`, gated on >=5 unique matches. Imbalance uses match-derived buy/sell USD; pace uses prior completed matched-volume windows. Historical 1m candle volume is not measured 30s taker flow. |
| F3 book pressure | Valid observed ticker BBO/sizes and spread, with observation ages and time weighting; no claim to full-book coverage. Optional perp data cannot substitute for missing required spot features. |
| F4 compression | Prior closed, already available candle history; require >=10 traded candles in the prior 15 completed minutes. Missing or late-fetched candles cannot become earlier features. |
| F5 market residual | Coin and BTC endpoints at the same causal cutoff, with beta provenance; a beta fallback remains visible. |

Freeze exact formulas, minimum baseline sample counts, missing-feature
policy and model inputs in the implementation contract. Do not automatically
add both decayed sums as extra fitted predictors beyond the declared five,
or choose between smooth and boxcar variants using test outcomes.

## 5. Extension, stage, score and reliability

Keep `score_0_100` (ordinal), `direction`, `stage`, `reliability`, input
windows and forward horizon as separate fields. Missing required evidence
produces an unavailable score and reason, not a fabricated neutral 50.
Feature coverage means measured feature coverage, not a count of connected
vendors; an optional missing perp feed does not automatically remove F1-F5.

For this prospective candidate retain the proposal's extension definition:

```text
E = abs(r_15m) / (sigma_1m * sqrt(15))
  + abs(r_60m) / (sigma_1m * sqrt(60))
```

This is a sum of two overlapping standardized lookbacks, not the number of
standard deviations of one independent return. Use causal midpoint prices
and declare missing extension history unavailable.

**`EXTENDED` is a hard exclusion from early-warning eligibility**, before
percentile ranking. For the candidate, `E > 3.5`, or an absolute daily move
`>10%` with measured trailing daily USD volume `<$1M`, sets `EXTENDED`. Missing
extension/liquidity data must not be treated as proof of eligibility. Stage
precedence is `EXTENDED`, then `FADING/ABSORBED`, then `CONFIRMED`, then
`IGNITING`; otherwise stage is unclassified. `E == 3.5` is not extended
by the E gate. Only complete-data `IGNITING` / `CONFIRMED` candidates enter
the early board, in separate UP/DOWN cohorts. Extended/fading rows may be
retained in shadow diagnostics or a separate risk-context view.

The original soft damper `exp(-max(0,E-3)^2/8)` retains 60.7% at E=5 and
32.5% at E=6 and cannot ensure exclusion. Dividing by 2 would retain 13.5%
and 1.1%; that remains a different hypothesis. The hard stage gate is the
chosen policy here. Do not infer the AURORA mockup's score of 18 from either
formula, or add another penalty/threshold search to this pilot.

Exact stage thresholds other than the extension/activity gates must be
frozen before fitting. Stage names do not establish "<30% of the move
elapsed"; that quantity requires future labels and cannot define live stage.

## Inputs and causality

`TargetEvaluator` accepts one product's strictly ordered, unique receipt-time
snapshots on a 5s grid. Each snapshot has coin and BTC bid/ask, original quote
timestamps, availability time, price-source flags, verified coverage,
deduplicated match count and match-derived USD volume. Quote age must be at
most 10s; both books must be positive and non-crossed. Last-trade fallbacks,
future receipts, unknown coverage and missing grid points are unavailable.
An empty but verified healthy bucket is a known zero, not a collection outage.
The recorder will have to prove these fields; accepting an input object
does not certify its provenance.

Counts/volume at grid time `t` describe `[t-5s,t)`. Forward counts exclude
trades exactly at the anchor: the first forward bucket also needs its
`trade_count_at_bucket_start`. Unknown boundary count means unavailable.
Forward quote states are at times strictly after the anchor. The anchor
itself supplies the starting price, spread and causal baseline.

`Baseline` supplies sigma from the preceding 60 closed 1m log returns,
ending exactly at the anchor's last completed minute, with availability at
or before the anchor. Require at least 60 returns; apply and expose the
0.0015 per-minute floor. Beta is frozen at the anchor with availability
and fallback flags. No rolling future beta/sigma can change the outcome path.
The evaluator checks metadata; candle/beta calculation belongs to the
later recorder/feature implementation.

Pace baselines are medians of 120 prior non-overlapping 30s trade-count and
USD-volume windows, ending exactly 30s before the anchor, available by it.
The current 30s window cannot contribute to its own reference median.
Zero medians cannot produce infinite ratios; at least one positive median
is required. An unavailable pace baseline invalidates onset, while a complete
forward impulse label may still be retained.

## Two separate labels

For direction `d = +1` (up) or `-1` (down), define the favorable residual:

```text
u(t) = d * [log(mid(t)/mid(anchor))
            - beta(anchor) * log(BTC(t)/BTC(anchor))]
```

For each horizon `H = 300s` or `900s`, `verified_forward` requires all of:

- Terminal `u(anchor+H) >= max(3*sigma*sqrt(H/60), 0.015, 3*entry_spread_fraction)`.
- Terminal favorable residual retains at least 60% of the largest favorable
  residual over the horizon. Include zero at the anchor and choose the first
  timestamp when peak values tie. Opposite-direction excursions cannot count
  as favorable merely because their absolute magnitude is large.
- At least three distinct, deduplicated matches strictly after the anchor
  and before the terminal boundary. Valid observed/carried quote coverage is
  required throughout. This is book-presence evidence, not executable fills.

The `0.015` threshold is explicitly in log-return units, approximately 1.5%.
The evaluator also retains adverse residual excursion, terminal return,
retention, first peak time and observed count for failed complete labels.
Rows without a move remain in the output; board entry is not a prerequisite.

`verified_onset` adds the following rules to `verified_forward`:

1. Signed 30s residual velocity divided by `sigma/sqrt(2)` crosses from below
   1 at the previous 5s grid point to at least 1 now. Both use the same causal
   anchor beta/sigma, avoiding a future normalization during evaluation.
2. At least five matches occur in the current 30s window. Its trade-count
   ratio or USD-volume ratio must be **strictly greater than 2** relative to
   its own prior median.
3. Starting from this candidate timestamp, take the first subsequent point
   within 180s with favorable residual at least `1.5*sigma` as the breakout.
4. After the candidate, the favorable residual never falls below zero through
   the full horizon, allowing `1e-12` floating-point tolerance.

The original proposal did not specify the cumulative breakout origin,
no-retrace endpoint or conflict resolution. This v1 fixes those choices:
each activity/velocity crossing supplies the breakout origin, and no-retrace
ends at `onset+H`. These are pilot definitions, not empirically optimal choices.
Onset means an **activity-confirmed threshold crossing**; it is not a claim
to identify the physical first tick of a move. A forward impulse can be real
while this stricter onset definition fails. Report that attrition explicitly.
The same numeric tolerance applies at inclusive/strict comparison boundaries.

## Maturity, event catalog and alert timing

`evaluate(anchor, baseline, direction, H, asof_ts_ms)` returns `pending`
until `anchor+H`, even if a partial path already looks successful. After
maturity, missing or invalid evidence gives `unavailable` and null labels.
`ready` means a complete label; the boolean can still be false. Future rows
after the horizon cannot change it. Endpoint slack is zero; the evaluator
does not interpolate, bridge gaps or select a favorable nearby observation.

`event_catalog` selects the earliest verified onset for each product,
direction and horizon; it suppresses further onsets in `[onset,onset+H)`.

# Timed volatility and activity baseline draft

The offline `build_timed_baseline(recorder, product, cutoff, asof=...)` API
takes a declared product and 5-second feature cutoff. It computes two
independent components from the timed input-evidence ledger.

- **Volatility and BTC beta.** Use 61 fresh, paired minute-close midpoints
  to form 60 log returns. The final close is
  `floor((cutoff_ms - 35000) / 60000) * 60000`. Use the coin's sample
  standard deviation and centered OLS against BTC. A flat BTC return series
  gives beta 1 with an explicit fallback flag. The trade receipt proof covers
  the preceding 5-second bucket at the first close through the final close.
- **Activity pace.** Use 120 half-open 30-second receipt windows ending at
  `cutoff_ms - 30000`. Median trade count and quote USD amount use first
  receipts. One proof chain within a single continuity epoch covers the
  full hour. A proved empty bin contributes zero to both medians. When a
  proof is missing, the component is unavailable. Maker side is irrelevant
  for these unsigned medians.

A missing benchmark close can block volatility while pace is ready. Each
ready component records the latest availability of the quote or trade
evidence it used, including the producer's payload completion. The
evidence as-of can precede the feature cutoff once a component's window
has closed. The plan must already be recorded by that as-of, and the as-of
must have elapsed on the recorder clock. The builder reads the clock again
after calculation and provenance hashing to record computation completion.
A later calculation with the same evidence as-of can use the same evidence
while having a later completion timestamp.

The result pins the inventory hash and its recorded time, the packet count
and SHA-256 of the known canonical packet prefix, and normalized source
and contract hashes. The draft is for offline component calculation.
Positive eligibility still requires feature, quote-health and publication
evidence. Throughput and availability on a real capture remain to be
measured.

The five new synthetic cases cover delayed proof completion, stable

# Timed instant-radar feature draft

The offline builder computes the five candidate inputs for one declared
product, 5-second feature cutoff `b`, and direction. The sign is +1 for up
and −1 for down. Each component has its own value or missingness reason.
The formulas use the candidate's fixed 30s and 180s windows. Sigma uses
the declared 0.0015 one-minute floor. The accompanying diagnostics retain
velocity, unsigned activity, cutoff spread, expansion, and raw signed
180s return for comparisons.

| Input | Computation |
| --- | --- |
| F1 acceleration | Direction times (30s midpoint log return minus one fifth of the preceding 150s log return) divided by floored sigma. Endpoints are `b-180s`, `b-30s`, and `b`. |
| F2 signed participation | Signed taker USD imbalance times log1p of 30s USD pace relative to the timed 120-bin median. Use at least five distinct first-receipt matches in `[b-30s,b)`, known maker sides inverted to taker sides, positive USD, and a positive baseline median. |
| F3 sampled pressure | Signed time-weighted mean of `(bid_size-ask_size)/(bid_size+ask_size)` over `[b-30s,b)`. |
| F4 active compression and expansion | Directional positive 30s velocity divided by `1 + log(max(high)/min(low))/(sigma*sqrt(15))`. |
| F5 market-relative movement | Signed `(coin_180s - beta * BTC_180s) / sigma` from synchronized midpoint endpoints. Retain the beta fallback flag. |

## F3 boundaries

Use fully processed book and transport events known by the evidence as-of,
ordered by local receipt and then sequence. Carry the last known state into
the window. At a receipt timestamp the final state in that millisecond applies
to the following segment. Events at `b` are excluded. Integrate only across
segments with a valid BBO, a matching active heartbeat, a quote no older than
10s, and a heartbeat no older than 5s. Age is measured from the original
receipt. Reaching an age limit at a segment's right edge is allowed. Any
positive-duration gap makes F3 unavailable. The denominator is 30 seconds.
The result measures sampled book pressure at logger receipts.

## F4 boundaries

Build high and low from actual first-receipt matches in 15 half-open,
UTC-aligned one-minute bins. The last bin ends at
`floor((b-30s)/60000)*60000` so its trades precede the expansion window.
One receipt-ID proof chain within a single continuity epoch must cover the
15-minute window. Count certified empty minutes separately and leave their
high and low absent. Require at least ten traded minutes. An incomplete
proof makes F4 unavailable. These are receipt-time trade candles.

## Timing and scope

A result carries the observation cutoff, explicit evidence as-of, each
ready component's evidence-availability time, actual baseline and feature
computation completion, and the timed baseline's evidence-prefix provenance.
Post-cutoff receipts cannot enter the formulas. A proof that becomes known
later can verify pre-cutoff observations at an explicitly later as-of. The
result identifies computation after the cutoff as diagnostic. An input
evidence timestamp does not include the subsequent baseline or feature
calculation. A consumer must use the recorded computation completion times.
This API produces offline evidence for the separately versioned decision
and grading design. A data-only pilot still needs to measure real
coverage and latency before a decision age limit or study freeze.

        isinstance(value, (float, int))

            terminal + tolerance >= threshold

            and retention + tolerance >= rules["minimum_peak_retention"]
            and trades >= rules["minimum_forward_matches"]
        )
        gross_bps = sign * (last.mid / first.mid - 1) * 10000
        spread_cost = (first.spread_bps + last.spread_bps) / 2
        result.update(
            status="ready",
            reason=None,
            verified_forward=verified,

            sigma_floor_applied=baseline.sigma_1m < sigma,

                ]

                    reference_path, key=lambda pair: pair[1]
                )
                result.update(
                    pre_window_peak_ts_ms=reference_peak_ts,
                    pre_window_peak_residual_log=reference_peak,
                )

            type(value) is not int
            for value in (
                baseline.pace_window_count,
                baseline.pace_end_ts_ms,
                baseline.pace_available_ts_ms,
            )

            return {
                **result,
                "onset_status": "unavailable",
                "onset_reason": "invalid_pace_metadata",

            reason = reason or "pace_warming"

            baseline.pace_end_ts_ms != anchor - velocity_ms
            or baseline.pace_available_ts_ms > anchor

            reason = reason or "future_pace_baseline"

            value > 0 for value in medians

            return {**result, "onset_status": "unavailable", "onset_reason": reason}

            anchor - velocity_ms, anchor, baseline.beta, sign

            anchor - velocity_ms - step, anchor - step, baseline.beta, sign

            self.rows[ts] for ts in range(anchor - velocity_ms + step, anchor + 1, step)
        ]
        recent_trades = sum(row.trade_count for row in recent)

            (
                value / median
                for value, median in zip((recent_trades, recent_volume), medians)
                if median > 0

            default=0,
        )
        candidate = (
            previous_velocity < onset["velocity_crossing_sigma"] - tolerance
            and current_velocity + tolerance >= onset["velocity_crossing_sigma"]
            and recent_trades >= onset["minimum_matches_30s"]
            and pace > onset["pace_multiple_strictly_greater_than"] + tolerance
        )
        breakout_ts = next(
            (
                ts
                for ts, value in path[1:]
                if ts <= anchor + onset["breakout_deadline_seconds"] * 1000
                and value + tolerance >= onset["breakout_sigma"] * sigma
            ),

        )
        no_retrace = adverse >= -onset["no_retrace_tolerance_log"]
        result.update(
            onset_status="ready",
            onset_reason=None,
            onset_candidate=candidate,
            previous_velocity_sigma=previous_velocity,
            velocity_sigma=current_velocity,
            pace_multiple=pace,
            recent_trade_count=recent_trades,
            breakout_ts_ms=breakout_ts,
            no_retrace=no_retrace,
            verified_onset=bool(
                candidate and verified and breakout_ts is not None and no_retrace
            ),
        )
        return result

    def alert_timing(self, label, publish_ts_ms):
        """Retrospective event association; never a live stage or input."""
        if self.is_v2:
            return self._v2_alert_timing(label, publish_ts_ms, strict_trigger=True)
        if type(publish_ts_ms) is not int:
            raise ValueError("publication timestamp must be an integer")
        if (
            label.get("contract_sha256") != self.contract_sha256
            or label.get("product_id") != self.product_id
            or not label.get("verified_onset")
        ):
            raise ValueError(
                "a verified onset from this contract and product is required"
            )
        anchor = label["anchor_ts_ms"]
        rules = self.cfg["events"]
        if (
            not anchor - rules["prediction_lead_limit_seconds"] * 1000
            <= publish_ts_ms
            <= label["label_maturity_ts_ms"]
        ):
            return dict(evaluation_only=True, timing="outside_event_window")
        # Backward as-of lookup only. Never use the next grid point at publication.
        ts = publish_ts_ms // self.cfg["grid_ms"] * self.cfg["grid_ms"]
        reason = self._quality(ts, ts)
        if reason:
            return dict(evaluation_only=True, timing="unavailable", reason=reason)
        row = self.rows[ts]
        if (
            max(publish_ts_ms - row.quote_ts_ms, publish_ts_ms - row.btc_quote_ts_ms)
            > self.cfg["max_quote_age_ms"]
        ):
            return dict(
                evaluation_only=True, timing="unavailable", reason="stale_alert_quote"
            )
        sign = 1 if label["direction"] == "up" else -1
        consumed = (
            self._residual(anchor, ts, label["beta"], sign) / label["peak_residual_log"]
        )
        timing = (
            "pre_onset_prediction"
            if publish_ts_ms < anchor
            else (
                "early_detection"
                if publish_ts_ms <= label["peak_ts_ms"]
                and consumed
                < rules["early_detection_consumed_fraction_strictly_below"]
                - self.cfg["comparison_tolerance"]
                else "late_detection"
            )
        )
        return dict(
            evaluation_only=True,
            timing=timing,
            detection_delay_seconds=(publish_ts_ms - anchor) / 1000,
            lead_to_peak_seconds=(label["peak_ts_ms"] - publish_ts_ms) / 1000,
            consumed_fraction=consumed,
            price_snapshot_ts_ms=ts,
        )

    def _v2_alert_timing(self, label, publish_ts_ms, strict_trigger):

            raise ValueError("publication timestamp must be an integer")
        required = "verified_onset" if strict_trigger else "verified_forward"
        if (
            label.get("contract_sha256") != self.contract_sha256

            or label.get("product_id") != self.product_id

            or label.get(required) is not True
        ):
            raise ValueError(
                "a complete verified label from this evaluator is required"
            )
        peak = label.get("peak_residual_log")
        if not finite(peak) or peak <= 0:
            raise ValueError("a positive finite forward peak is required")
        anchor = label["anchor_ts_ms"]
        lead_ms = self.cfg["events"]["prediction_lead_limit_seconds"] * 1000
        if not anchor - lead_ms <= publish_ts_ms <= label["label_maturity_ts_ms"]:
            return dict(evaluation_only=True, timing="outside_event_window")
        ts = publish_ts_ms // self.cfg["grid_ms"] * self.cfg["grid_ms"]

        if reason:
            return dict(evaluation_only=True, timing="unavailable", reason=reason)
        row = self.rows[ts]

                evaluation_only=True, timing="unavailable", reason="stale_alert_quote"
            )
        sign = 1 if label["direction"] == "up" else -1
        post_fraction = self._residual(anchor, ts, label["beta"], sign) / peak
        output = dict(
            evaluation_only=True,
            physical_onset_defined=False,
            post_anchor_consumed_fraction=post_fraction,
            post_anchor_reference_ts_ms=anchor,
            offset_from_anchor_seconds=(publish_ts_ms - anchor) / 1000,
            lead_to_forward_peak_seconds=(label["peak_ts_ms"] - publish_ts_ms) / 1000,
            price_snapshot_ts_ms=ts,
            pre_window_consumed_fraction=None,
            pre_window_reference_ts_ms=label.get("pre_window_reference_ts_ms"),
            pre_window_reference_is_physical_origin=False,
        )

            label.get("pre_window_reference_status") == "ready"

        ):

                )

            )

                label["pre_window_peak_ts_ms"] - publish_ts_ms

                )

                < self.cfg["events"]["early_detection_consumed_fraction_strictly_below"]
                - self.cfg["comparison_tolerance"]
            )
            output["timing"] = (
                "before_activity_trigger"
                if publish_ts_ms < anchor
                else (
                    "early_after_activity_trigger"
                    if early
                    else "late_after_activity_trigger"
                )
            )
            output["association_kind"] = "strict_activity_trigger"
        else:
            output["timing"] = (
                "before_forward_window_anchor"
                if publish_ts_ms < anchor
                else (
                    "within_forward_window_before_peak"
                    if publish_ts_ms <= label["peak_ts_ms"]
                    else "within_forward_window_after_peak"
                )
            )
            output["association_kind"] = "forward_window_not_physical_impulse"
        return output


def event_catalog(labels, *, target="verified_onset"):
    """Earliest verified event owns [onset,onset+H), per product/direction/H.

    This retrospective deduplication is separate from the live 15m per-coin
    alert cooldown. Unverified/pending rows never suppress another event.
    """
    labels = list(labels)
    if target not in ("verified_onset", "verified_forward"):
        raise ValueError("unsupported catalog target")
    selected, ends = [], {}
    hashes = {row["contract_sha256"] for row in labels}
    if len(hashes) > 1:
        raise ValueError("cannot mix target contracts")
    implementations = {row.get("evaluator_sha256") for row in labels}
    if len(implementations) > 1:
        raise ValueError("cannot mix evaluator implementations")
    for row in sorted(
        labels,
        key=lambda row: (
            row["anchor_ts_ms"],
            row["product_id"],
            row["direction"],
            row["horizon_seconds"],
        ),
    ):
        key = row["product_id"], row["direction"], row["horizon_seconds"]
        if (
            row.get(target) is True
            and row.get("status") == "ready"
            and row["anchor_ts_ms"] >= ends.get(key, -math.inf)
        ):
            selected.append(row)
            ends[key] = row["label_maturity_ts_ms"]
    return selected


def forward_event_catalog(labels):
    """Catalog forward windows independently of activity/no-retrace gates.

    These are deduplicated qualifying forecast windows, not a definition of
    physical move onset or a completed operational missed-move denominator.
    """
    labels = list(labels)
    if any(row.get("contract_version") != "instant-target-v2-draft" for row in labels):
        raise ValueError(
            "forward-window catalog requires labels from the explicit v2 draft"
        )
    return [
        {**row, "catalog_kind": "forward_window_not_physical_impulse"}
        for row in event_catalog(labels, target="verified_forward")
    ]

def _volatility(recorder, product, start, end, asof):
    if start < GRID_MS:
        return dict(status="unavailable", reason="insufficient_history")
    if end > asof:
        return dict(status="unavailable", reason="window_not_closed")

    availability, returns = [], {}
    for pid in dict.fromkeys((product, BENCHMARK)):
        # Include the bucket preceding the first close, as in micro_baseline.
        _, _, reason, available = recorder._trade_window(
            pid, start - GRID_MS, end, asof
        )
        if reason:
            return dict(status="unavailable", reason=reason, product_id=pid)
        availability.append(available)
        closes = []
        for cutoff in range(start, end + 1, MINUTE_MS):
            quote, available = recorder._quote_at(pid, cutoff, asof)
            if not quote["available"]:
                return dict(
                    status="unavailable",
                    reason=quote["reason"],
                    product_id=pid,
                    missing_close_ts_ms=cutoff,
                )
            bid, ask = float(quote["bid"]), float(quote["ask"])
            closes.append(math.log(bid + (ask - bid) / 2))
            availability.append(available)
        returns[pid] = [b - a for a, b in zip(closes, closes[1:])]

    coin, btc = returns[product], returns[BENCHMARK]
    coin_mean, btc_mean = statistics.fmean(coin), statistics.fmean(btc)
    variance = sum((value - btc_mean) ** 2 for value in btc)
    fallback = variance == 0
    beta = (
        1.0
        if fallback
        else (
            sum((a - coin_mean) * (b - btc_mean) for a, b in zip(coin, btc)) / variance
        )
    )
    sigma = statistics.stdev(coin)
    if not math.isfinite(beta) or not math.isfinite(sigma):
        return dict(status="unavailable", reason="nonfinite_baseline")
    return dict(
        status="ready",
        inputs_available_ts_ms=max(availability),
        return_count=len(coin),
        sigma_1m=sigma,
        beta=beta,
        beta_fallback=fallback,
    )


def _pace(recorder, product, start, end, asof):
    if start < 0:
        return dict(status="unavailable", reason="insufficient_history")
    if end > asof:
        return dict(status="unavailable", reason="window_not_closed")
    # A single certified chain covers the entire interval, including every
    # empty subwindow. Receipts are then assigned to half-open 30s bins.
    _, trades, reason, available = recorder._trade_window(product, start, end, asof)
    if reason:
        return dict(status="unavailable", reason=reason, product_id=product)
    counts, amounts = [0] * PACE_COUNT, [Decimal(0)] * PACE_COUNT
    try:
        if recorder._index is None:
            for trade in trades:
                index = (trade.receipt_ts_ms - start) // PACE_MS
                counts[index] += 1
                amounts[index] += Decimal(trade.price) * Decimal(trade.size)
        else:
            counts, amounts = recorder._index.pace_bins(product, start, end, PACE_MS)
        median = statistics.median(amounts)
    except DecimalException:
        return dict(status="unavailable", reason="invalid_quote_amount")
    if not median.is_finite():
        return dict(status="unavailable", reason="invalid_quote_amount")
    return dict(
        status="ready",
        inputs_available_ts_ms=available,
        window_count=PACE_COUNT,
        pace_trade_median_30s=statistics.median(counts),
        pace_quote_usd_median_30s=str(median),
    )


def build_timed_baseline(recorder, product, cutoff, *, asof):
    """Assess independent volatility and pace components for a declared slot.

    Windows end before cutoff, so a baseline may finish ahead of the slot.
    The evidence as-of is an input limit and completion uses the recorder clock.
    """
    _integer(cutoff)
    _integer(asof)
    if recorder.failed:
        raise ValueError("failed evidence recorder")
    if product not in recorder.products or cutoff not in recorder.cutoffs:
        raise ValueError("product/cutoff outside declared inventory")
    if asof < recorder.inventory_recorded_ts_ms:
        raise ValueError("inventory was not yet recorded at baseline evidence time")
    if asof > recorder._now():
        raise ValueError("baseline evidence time has not been reached")

    sigma_end = (cutoff - 35000) // MINUTE_MS * MINUTE_MS
    sigma_start = sigma_end - RETURN_COUNT * MINUTE_MS
    pace_end = cutoff - PACE_MS
    pace_start = pace_end - PACE_COUNT * PACE_MS
    volatility = _volatility(recorder, product, sigma_start, sigma_end, asof)
    pace = _pace(recorder, product, pace_start, pace_end, asof)
    known_packets, prefix, _ = recorder._prefix_at(asof)

    result = dict(
        version=VERSION,
        product_id=product,
        benchmark=BENCHMARK,
        feature_cutoff_ts_ms=cutoff,
        knowledge_asof_ts_ms=asof,
        sigma_start_ts_ms=sigma_start,
        sigma_end_ts_ms=sigma_end,
        pace_start_ts_ms=pace_start,
        pace_end_ts_ms=pace_end,
        volatility=volatility,
        pace=pace,
        inventory_sha256=recorder.inventory_sha256,
        inventory_recorded_ts_ms=recorder.inventory_recorded_ts_ms,
        known_packet_count=known_packets,
        known_packet_prefix_sha256=prefix,
        input_implementation_sha256=recorder.source_sha256,
        input_contract_sha256=recorder.contract_sha256,

        book_contract_sha256=recorder.book.contract_sha256,

        monitor_contract_sha256=recorder.book.monitor.contract_sha256,
        builder_sha256=_sha(Path(__file__).read_text(encoding="utf-8").encode("utf-8")),
        scope="offline_baseline_components",
        fitting_allowed=False,
    )
    result["computation_completed_ts_ms"] = recorder._now()
    return result

def _missing(reason, **details):

def _value(value, *known, **details):

def _price_point(recorder, product, cutoff, asof):
    if cutoff < 0:
        return _missing(
            "insufficient_history", product_id=product, endpoint_ts_ms=cutoff
        )
    quote, known = recorder._quote_at(product, cutoff, asof)
    if not quote["available"]:
        return _missing(quote["reason"], product_id=product, endpoint_ts_ms=cutoff)
    bid, ask = float(quote["bid"]), float(quote["ask"])
    mid = bid + (ask - bid) / 2

def _move(start, stop):
    for point in (start, stop):
        if point["status"] != "ready":
            return point
    return _value(
        stop["value"] - start["value"],
        start["evidence_available_ts_ms"],
        stop["evidence_available_ts_ms"],
    )


def _participation(recorder, product, start, end, asof, pace, sign):
    if start < 0:
        return _missing("insufficient_history"), {}
    _, trades, reason, known = recorder._trade_window(product, start, end, asof)
    if reason:
        return _missing(reason), {}

    counts = dict(verified_match_count=len(trades))
    try:
        amounts = {"buy": Decimal(0), "sell": Decimal(0), None: Decimal(0)}
        for trade in trades:
            amounts[trade.taker_side] += Decimal(trade.price) * Decimal(trade.size)
        total = sum(amounts.values())
        if not total.is_finite():
            return _missing("invalid_quote_amount"), counts
        counts["verified_quote_usd"] = str(total)
        counts["unknown_side_usd"] = str(amounts[None])
        if pace["status"] != "ready":
            return (
                _missing("pace_baseline_unavailable", baseline_reason=pace["reason"]),
                counts,

        median = Decimal(pace["pace_quote_usd_median_30s"])
        if median <= 0:
            return _missing("nonpositive_pace_median"), counts
        ratio = float(total / median)
        if not math.isfinite(ratio) or (total > 0 and ratio == 0):
            return _missing("invalid_volume_pace"), counts
        counts["unsigned_volume_pace"] = ratio
        if len(trades) < MIN_MATCHES:
            return _missing("too_few_matches"), counts
        if any(trade.taker_side is None for trade in trades):
            return _missing("unknown_match_side"), counts

            return _missing("nonpositive_window_quote_amount"), counts
        imbalance = float((amounts["buy"] - amounts["sell"]) / total)
    except DecimalException:
        return _missing("invalid_quote_amount"), counts
    return (
        _value(
            sign * imbalance * math.log1p(ratio), known, pace["inputs_available_ts_ms"]
        ),
        counts,
    )


def _book_pressure(recorder, product, start, end, asof, sign):

    # the last carried state; overwriting a same-ms key keeps the last event.

    state = heartbeat = None
    known = end
    integral = 0.0

            )
        bid_size, ask_size = map(Decimal, state.best[2:])
        pressure = float((bid_size - ask_size) / (bid_size + ask_size))
        integral += pressure * (right - left)
        known = max(
            known,
            recorder._packet_by_seq[state.receipt_seq].available_ts_ms,
            recorder._packet_by_seq[heartbeat["receipt_seq"]].available_ts_ms,
        )
    return _value(sign * integral / (end - start), known)


def _candle_range(recorder, product, start, end, asof):
    if start < 0:
        return _missing("insufficient_history")
    _, trades, reason, known = recorder._trade_window(product, start, end, asof)
    if reason:
        return _missing(reason)
    candles = {}
    for trade in trades:
        bucket = trade.receipt_ts_ms // MINUTE_MS
        price = Decimal(trade.price)
        low, high = candles.get(bucket, (price, price))
        candles[bucket] = min(low, price), max(high, price)
    counts = dict(
        traded_candles=len(candles), verified_empty_candles=CANDLE_COUNT - len(candles)
    )
    if len(candles) < MIN_TRADED_CANDLES:
        return _missing("too_few_traded_candles", **counts)
    low = min(candle[0] for candle in candles.values())
    high = max(candle[1] for candle in candles.values())
    try:
        log_range = float(high.ln() - low.ln())
    except DecimalException:
        return _missing("invalid_candle_range", **counts)
    return _value(log_range, known, **counts)


def _scaled_velocity(move, baseline, sigma, sign):
    if move["status"] != "ready":
        return move
    if baseline["status"] != "ready":
        return _missing(
            "volatility_baseline_unavailable", baseline_reason=baseline["reason"]
        )
    return _value(
        sign * move["value"] / sigma,
        move["evidence_available_ts_ms"],
        baseline["inputs_available_ts_ms"],
    )


def _acceleration(velocity, prior, sigma, sign):
    for component in (velocity, prior):
        if component["status"] != "ready":
            return component
    return _value(
        velocity["value"] - sign * prior["value"] / (5 * sigma),
        velocity["evidence_available_ts_ms"],
        prior["evidence_available_ts_ms"],
    )


def _compression(velocity, candles, sigma):
    for component in (velocity, candles):
        if component["status"] != "ready":
            return component
    expansion = max(velocity["value"], 0)
    return _value(
        expansion / (1 + candles["value"] / (sigma * math.sqrt(CANDLE_COUNT))),
        velocity["evidence_available_ts_ms"],
        candles["evidence_available_ts_ms"],
    )


def _market_relative(coin_move, btc_move, baseline, sigma, sign):
    for component in (coin_move, btc_move):
        if component["status"] != "ready":
            return component
    if baseline["status"] != "ready":
        return _missing(
            "volatility_baseline_unavailable", baseline_reason=baseline["reason"]
        )
    return _value(
        sign * (coin_move["value"] - baseline["beta"] * btc_move["value"]) / sigma,
        coin_move["evidence_available_ts_ms"],
        btc_move["evidence_available_ts_ms"],
        baseline["inputs_available_ts_ms"],
        beta_fallback=baseline["beta_fallback"],
    )


def build_timed_features(recorder, product, cutoff, direction, *, asof):
    """Calculate a single offline feature row with explicit evidence and clocks."""
    if direction not in DIRECTIONS:
        raise ValueError("direction must be up or down")
    if type(cutoff) is not int or type(asof) is not int or asof < cutoff:
        raise ValueError("feature as-of must be at or after the integer cutoff")
    baseline = build_timed_baseline(recorder, product, cutoff, asof=asof)
    vol, pace = baseline["volatility"], baseline["pace"]
    sigma = max(vol["sigma_1m"], SIGMA_FLOOR) if vol["status"] == "ready" else None
    sign = 1 if direction == "up" else -1
    slow, fast = cutoff - SLOW_MS, cutoff - PACE_MS

    start, middle, end = (
        _price_point(recorder, product, t, asof) for t in (slow, fast, cutoff)
    )
    recent, prior = _move(middle, end), _move(start, middle)
    coin = _move(start, end)
    btc = (
        coin
        if product == BENCHMARK
        else _move(
            _price_point(recorder, BENCHMARK, slow, asof),
            _price_point(recorder, BENCHMARK, cutoff, asof),
        )
    )
    candle_end = fast // MINUTE_MS * MINUTE_MS
    candle_start = candle_end - CANDLE_COUNT * MINUTE_MS
    candles = _candle_range(recorder, product, candle_start, candle_end, asof)
    velocity = _scaled_velocity(recent, vol, sigma, sign)
    flow, activity = _participation(recorder, product, fast, cutoff, asof, pace, sign)
    features = {
        "F1": _acceleration(velocity, prior, sigma, sign),
        "F2": flow,
        "F3": _book_pressure(recorder, product, fast, cutoff, asof, sign),
        "F4": _compression(velocity, candles, sigma),
        "F5": _market_relative(coin, btc, vol, sigma, sign),
    }

    row = dict(
        version=VERSION,
        product_id=product,
        direction=direction,
        feature_cutoff_ts_ms=cutoff,
        knowledge_asof_ts_ms=asof,
        baseline=baseline,
        sigma_used=sigma,
        candle_start_ts_ms=candle_start,
        candle_end_ts_ms=candle_end,
        features=features,
        controls=dict(
            current_velocity_sigma=velocity,
            unsigned_activity=activity,
            cutoff_spread_bps=end.get("spread_bps"),
            candle_range=candles,
            expansion=(
                max(velocity["value"], 0) if velocity["status"] == "ready" else None
            ),
            raw_signed_180s=sign * coin["value"] if coin["status"] == "ready" else None,
        ),
        builder_sha256=_sha(Path(__file__).read_text(encoding="utf-8").encode("utf-8")),
        scope="offline_feature_components",
        fitting_allowed=False,
    )
    row["computation_completed_ts_ms"] = recorder._now()
    row["later_diagnostic"] = row["computation_completed_ts_ms"] > cutoff
    return row

# Delayed decision evidence and later cutoff grading

The offline module `instant_delayed_decision.py` adds two draft record versions,
`instant-delayed-decision-v1-draft` and `instant-later-cutoff-grade-v1-draft`.

## Decision and publication clocks

`prepare_decision` builds a timed F1–F5 row for a declared slot. It freezes
canonical feature and paired anchor-quote bytes and measures preparation
completion after serialization. The snapshot retains the observation cutoff
`b`, evidence as-of, feature completion, and decision preparation completion.
All observations used in the feature values precede `b`. A later evidence
as-of can admit later proof of a pre-cutoff interval. Unavailable features
stay unavailable in that snapshot.

`observe_publication` associates the snapshot with a caller-supplied
publication identity and timestamp. It validates this chronology and
measures when the observation was recorded:

```text
b <= decision evidence as-of <= feature computation completed
  <= snapshot preparation completed <= observed publication <= observation recorded
```

The observation uses the feature computation completion as the time all
computed inputs could be available, while the feature row preserves the
individual evidence clocks. The supplied timestamp is an observation
assertion. Durable delivery and scoring are separate work. An optional
diagnostic age bound uses the existing publication helper. Study selection
of an age limit awaits measured source and processing latency.

## Later grading

`grade_publication` returns an independent diagnostic view at a later
as-of. It checks the decision's inventory, ledger prefix and source provenance,
and records the grading prefix and decision digest. The requested as-of
must have elapsed and include the publication observation. It returns
`pending` through the period before `b+H`, with `H` equal to 300 or 900
seconds. Once mature, absent or contradicted evidence returns
`unavailable` with a null outcome. A mature grade with adequate evidence
returns a Boolean `cutoff_verified_forward`.

For this draft the published snapshot needs all five features available.
Grading rechecks the frozen inputs' trade proof windows and both products'
receipt-ID coverage from the 5s bucket preceding the anchor through
the horizon. Later conflicts in these windows can invalidate a grade.

horizon is mature. This conservative boundary keeps a monitor result
from reaching grading before the recorder finishes the packet. The
original snapshot and earlier evidence views remain independently
reproducible.

The outcome uses paired, fresh receipt-time midpoints at each 5s endpoint,
the frozen anchor and volatility/beta, and first-receipt matches strictly
inside `(b,b+H)`. The terminal residual, spread and sigma threshold, peak
retention and forward match floor follow the pinned V2 impulse rules.
A contradiction in the anchor quote vetoes grading. A different outcome
as-of can change the grade's evidence without substituting new decision
inputs. Publication time is reported separately from the fixed cutoff
horizon. The grade includes whether publication preceded the horizon end.

This draft measures a fixed-cutoff outcome on scoped evidence. A later
publication-anchored quote benchmark needs its own entry and maturity

## Primary pointwise outcomes stay fixed

`association_report(labels, alerts, asof_ts_ms=...)` takes complete v2 labels
(`instant-target-v2-draft`, one contract hash and one evaluator hash) and alert
records with `publication_id`, `product_id`, `direction`, `anchor_ts_ms`,
`inputs_available_ts_ms` and `published_ts_ms`. For each alert and **each
horizon separately** it looks up `verified_forward` at the alert's declared
decision-cutoff anchor:

| Label at (product, direction, horizon, anchor) | Primary outcome |
|---|---|
| `ready`, `verified_forward` True | `success` |
| `ready`, `verified_forward` False | `failure` |
| `pending`, `unavailable` or absent | `ungradable`, reason retained, outside both denominators |

`success_rate_among_graded` divides by success plus failure only;
`graded_fraction` shows how much of the alert set that covers. A ready label
whose outcome is not a boolean, an incomplete label carrying an outcome, a
ready label maturing after the as-of, a v1 label, mixed provenance, duplicate
identities, off-grid anchors and malformed rows are rejected. Nothing missing
is coerced to false.

Alerts carry no horizon: every alert is graded in both tables because the
study evaluates 300s and 900s outcomes separately. `publication_id` is the
retained identity and must be unique.

## Forward-window coverage diagnostic

Windows are the deduplicated verified forward windows from
`instant_diagnostics.forward_catalog_report`, so left-censor flags come from
the existing reporting layer unchanged. Per product, direction and horizon,
windows are sorted by anchor and each receives the earliest unused alert with
actual publication in `[anchor - 180s, forward_peak]`, ordering alerts by
`published_ts_ms` and then `publication_id`. One alert covers at most one
window per horizon; the same alert may cover a 5m window and a 15m window.
Unmatched windows and alerts are listed. Rates are named
`uncovered_forward_window_rate` and `conditional_catalog_coverage_rate`;
`physical_missed_move_rate` is always null and `association_kind` is
`forward_window_not_physical_impulse`.

The report counts `failed_alerts_that_cover_a_forward_window` and states
`association_alters_primary_outcomes: false`. Three evaluator-graded tests
make this executable: a 5m alert whose move begins at +340s and a 15m alert
whose move begins at +940s both cover the later catalog window and both remain
pointwise failures; an alert published at a completed window's endpoint,
where the forward peak equals maturity, covers that window and is still a
failure at its own anchor.

**Known property for review.** Earliest-unused assignment is the declared
rule from the freeze candidate, implemented literally. It is not a maximum
matching: when a window's lead edge overlaps the previous window's tail, the
greedy choice can leave a later window uncovered although a different pairing
would cover both. The rule is transparent and deterministic; changing it is a
contract amendment, not a tuning step.

## Exposure from explicit eligibility records

`exposure_report(labels, eligibility, asof_ts_ms=..., ranker_slots=None)` takes
one record per product and 5s cutoff with four explicit booleans:
`universe_member`, `warmup_complete`, `health_verified`, `candidate_eligible`.
A slot is **decision-eligible** when all four hold. Exclusions are counted by
the first failing flag in that order. Duplicate or overlapping slots, missing
or non-boolean flags, off-grid cutoffs and cutoffs after the as-of are rejected.

- `decision_eligible_product_hours` sums eligible slots across products.
  `board_wall_clock_hours_with_any_eligible_product` counts the union of
  cutoffs with at least one eligible product; it is never the sum. A separate
  union counts cutoffs with any verified health.
- `universe_size_by_cutoff` lists the universe size at every cutoff, with
  first, last, minimum and maximum, so a shrinking universe stays visible.
- Per horizon, an eligible slot is **retrospectively gradable** only if its
  trailing horizon matured by the as-of and both direction labels are
  `ready`. Immature, missing and incomplete cases are counted by reason.
  The decision-eligible counts never depend on labels, so missing future
  data cannot erase the decision universe.
- `comparison` distinguishes identical complete rows (eligible, gradable and
  scored by every supplied ranker) from each ranker's natural coverage of
  eligible and of gradable slots. Ranker slots must be recorded slots.

Eligibility flags are caller assertions. This module does not verify
health, warmup or universe membership itself; the monitor, book adapter and
future recorder supply that evidence under their own contracts.

Each alert retains its input anchor, input availability and actual
publication separately, with derived `input_lag_ms`, `publication_delay_ms`
and `inputs_known_at_anchor`. Records must satisfy anchor <= inputs available

as known at an earlier anchor. The primary outcome is graded at the declared

## Book adapter

`scripts/scoring_experiment/instant_book.py` implements `BookReplay` under
[a separate versioned contract](INSTANT_BOOK_CONTRACT_V1_DRAFT.json). It
consumes the existing receipt envelope, using the monitor for bounded packet,
clock, subscription and connection checks. It performs no network access.

The official Exchange channel specification defines a complete initial
snapshot, then absolute price-level sizes, with zero deleting a level.
`level2_batch` uses that schema in 50ms batches, with its exchange time referring
to the latest event in the batch.
[Coinbase channel reference](https://docs.cdp.coinbase.com/exchange/websocket-feed/channels).
Our implementation uses receipt order and preserves exchange time as metadata.
It does not assume contiguous exchange sequence numbers for selected channels.

- Acknowledged `level2_batch`, `matches` and `heartbeat` memberships are required
  for each declared product. A valid book starts from a fresh snapshot.
- Decimal prices/sizes retain their exact level identity. Amounts must be
  finite, nonnegative and representable for downstream floating readers;
  prices and resting snapshot sizes must be positive. Duplicate snapshot
  prices, empty sides, and final locked/crossed books are unavailable.
- Update batches apply atomically. Absolute sizes replace prior quantities;
  zero deletes; repeated changes at a price use the final change. Temporary
  intrabatch crossings do not create synthetic intermediate quotes or peaks.
- An invalid batch discards book continuity until a new valid snapshot.
  Connection/subscription loss, queue/transport/audit failures require a fresh
  acknowledgement and snapshot. A heartbeat gap above 5s requires a snapshot.
  Stale heartbeat and stale quote are separately reported.
- Last actual book observation, whole-book change and BBO change times remain
  distinct. A real deep-level or no-op update is an observation; only an actual
  level/BBO change moves the corresponding change clock. Heartbeats and ticker
  packets never refresh the book observation. The original 10s observation TTL
  remains in force, with exactly 10s allowed.
- Processing completion is measured after book construction. Read-only frozen
  results retain that availability time and source/contract/monitor hashes.
  Queries filter receipt strictly before cutoff and knowledge at or before
  the requested as-of. Explicit later diagnostics do not rewrite earlier as-of
  views. A later failure becomes visible only when known.

`view(product, cutoff, asof=...)` describes the last **fully processed** state
then known. An in-flight/queued update is not certified by an older visible
quote. `full_feed_verified` and `target_sample_adapter_supported` stay false.
This is not a queue-durability certificate, a continuous F3 integral, a full
intrabatch path, or a bridge that silently substitutes books into legacy
`ReplayStore`/`Sample`. Historical replay uses today's processing clock;
the injected clock in synthetic tests is not authenticated historical evidence.

At most 100,000 levels per product are allowed. Packet/product limits inherit
the monitor's 100,000 packets/32 products. This is a bounded offline prototype,
not a claim that a multi-product one-hour book capture fits those limits.
Version and test streaming persistence/rotation, real connection identities,
queue-loss reporting and processing/durable-publication evidence before a
source pilot. Do not raise a cap merely to make an old sample pass.

## What is implemented

`scripts/scoring_experiment/instant_coverage.py` processes saved public
WebSocket envelopes, with no sockets, backend imports or account access.
The new [monitor contract](INSTANT_COVERAGE_CONTRACT_V1_DRAFT.json) is separate
from the existing recorder and target contracts.

- Subscription membership is acknowledged per product and connection.
  Disconnects, errors, queue-overflow markers and lost required subscriptions
  invalidate continuity. A new acknowledgement and heartbeat baseline are
  required before a fresh interval can pass.
- Consecutive heartbeat watermarks define an expected set of trade IDs.
  It must equal the unique live-match IDs received in the half-open receipt
  interval. Missing IDs, unexpected IDs, regressing watermarks, gaps above
  5 seconds and ID spans above 100,000 fail that interval. Boundary collisions
  are conservative failures, not reassigned executions.
- Identity is product plus trade ID, first receipt wins. Decimal amounts are
  compared exactly for duplicate conflicts. Later conflicting duplicates veto
  overlapping evidence only when the conflict is known. REST, `last_match`
  and ticker sizes cannot repair live receipt counts.
- Local logger sequence must be contiguous; receipt and monotonic clocks
  cannot regress. Missing monotonic metadata or invalid amounts fail the
  artifact closed. At most 100,000 packets and 32 declared products are
  accepted; a truncated prefix is never called a complete input.
- Evidence availability uses the measured **completion** of processing and
  at least receipt plus 1ms. A message received exactly at a cutoff is outside
  observations strictly before that cutoff. CLI replay uses the current clock,
  never the historical receipt time. The injectable clock is for testing and
  remains a trusted assertion, not authenticated historical evidence.

`assess(product, start, end, asof=...)` keeps `receipt_id_check_passed`,
`transport_observed_ok` and quote quality separate. A passing ID check is
conditional on the recorded heartbeat range and receipt attribution. It
does **not** establish exchange-event completeness before a lagging watermark,
independent REST agreement, upstream queue latency, durable consumer writes,
or unobserved book continuity. `full_feed_verified` and quote verification
remain false. Proofs are intentionally **not** a `ReplayStore.add_health`
drop-in. The legacy writer's omitted source tag is interpreted as WebSocket;
a reshaped recovery must retain an explicit non-WebSocket source. Shape and
hash metadata do not authenticate provenance.

The bounded CLI creates a new output directory outside the input directory,
hashes the unchanged input before/after and writes a report plus ID-check
records. Failures retain `FAILURE.json`; existing output is refused.


missing quotes or no proven trade interval. Empty observations do not shrink

## Processing and component evidence

Packet evidence stores receipt sequence/time, measured processing start and
completion, availability at least receipt plus 1ms, and canonical envelope
hash. Unique live-match rows retain their first receipt, packet completion,
connection and continuity epoch, exact Decimal price/size and inverted maker
side. Unknown sides remain separate. REST recovery, ticker sizes and
`last_match` do not create execution rows; duplicates never move first receipt.
Completion measures book/trade payload processing, not disk commit or durable
publication. Injected clocks remain trusted synthetic-test inputs.

Coin and BTC evidence are computed separately:

- The quote component uses the existing last processed book view and original
  observation age. Its selected state and heartbeat must also have completed
  this producer's processing by the requested knowledge time. A heartbeat
  cannot refresh quote age; an unfinished quote cannot borrow book-only
  completion. No continuous 30s F3 path is certified.
- The 30s trade component uses exact receipt-ID proofs plus completed trade
  rows, scoped to the verifying connection and continuity epoch. A first
  receipt at a shared-millisecond reset stays in its original epoch and cannot
  be certified by the following epoch. Known raw counts remain diagnostic;
  verified counts/amounts stay null without these checks. Only a proven empty
  receipt window becomes zero. Unknown taker side prevents signed-flow use.

`checkpoint(cutoff)` freezes the full product/direction cohort with component
knowledge at that cutoff and a separately measured assessment-completion
time. Returned rows are frozen and their nested component payload is serialized;
decoded copies cannot mutate them. Later packets, conflicts or failures never
rewrite the frozen cohort. Local logger/clock corruption still fails the whole
artifact closed, rather than exporting earlier rows from a failed run.

`component_evidence(product, cutoff, asof=later)` is a separately marked later

def _input_trade_windows(row):

def _mid(quote):
    bid, ask = float(quote["bid"]), float(quote["ask"])
    return bid + (ask - bid) / 2


def _forward_metrics(quotes, product, direction, volatility, trades, horizon, cfg):
    first_coin, first_btc = (_mid(quotes[0][pid]) for pid in (product, BENCHMARK))
    sign = 1 if direction == "up" else -1
    path = [
        sign
        * (
            (math.log(_mid(point[product])) - math.log(first_coin))
            - volatility["beta"]
            * (math.log(_mid(point[BENCHMARK])) - math.log(first_btc))
        )
        for point in quotes
    ]
    sigma = max(volatility["sigma_1m"], cfg["sigma_floor_1m"])
    rules = cfg["impulse"]
    entry = quotes[0][product]
    spread = (float(entry["ask"]) - float(entry["bid"])) / first_coin
    required = max(
        rules["terminal_sigma_multiple"] * sigma * math.sqrt(horizon / 60),
        rules["minimum_terminal_log_return"],
        rules["entry_spread_multiple"] * spread,
    )
    terminal, peak = path[-1], max(path)
    retention = terminal / peak if peak > 0 else None
    tolerance = cfg["comparison_tolerance"]
    verified = (
        terminal + tolerance >= required
        and retention is not None
        and retention + tolerance >= rules["minimum_peak_retention"]
        and trades >= rules["minimum_forward_matches"]
    )
    return dict(
        status="ready",
        reason=None,
        cutoff_verified_forward=verified,
        terminal_residual_log=terminal,
        required_terminal_log=required,
        peak_residual_log=peak,
        retention=retention,
        forward_match_count=trades,
    )

"""Offline publication-clock diagnostics. No target labels or live decisions."""

from dataclasses import dataclass
import hashlib
import json
from pathlib import Path

from instant_coverage import _integer


VERSION = "instant-publication-clock-v1-draft"
CONTRACT = (
    Path(__file__).resolve().parents[2]
    / "docs/research/INSTANT_PUBLICATION_CONTRACT_V1_DRAFT.json"
)


@dataclass(frozen=True)
class PublicationTiming:
    version: str
    publication_id: str
    product_id: str
    feature_cutoff_ts_ms: int
    inputs_available_ts_ms: int
    decision_completed_ts_ms: int
    published_ts_ms: int
    feature_age_at_publication_ms: int
    verification_lag_from_cutoff_ms: int
    decision_to_publication_ms: int
    legacy_cutoff_inputs_compatible: bool
    publication_target_amendment_required: bool
    max_feature_age_ms: int | None
    within_declared_feature_age: bool | None
    readiness_reason: str
    eligible_for_fitting: bool
    contract_sha256: str
    implementation_sha256: str


def publication_timing(
    *,
    publication_id,
    product_id,
    feature_cutoff,
    inputs_available,
    decision_completed,
    published,
    max_feature_age_ms=None
):
    """Record explicit clocks; a supplied age bound is not study approval.

    Caller clocks/provenance remain trusted. A later proof is representable
    here but must never be put into an old Sample as if known at its cutoff.
    This validates chronology only; it does not prove durable publication.
    """
    raw = CONTRACT.read_text(encoding="utf-8").encode("utf-8")
    cfg = json.loads(raw)
    if (
        cfg["version"] != VERSION
        or cfg["grid_ms"] != 5000
        or cfg["fitting_allowed"] is not False
    ):
        raise ValueError("publication contract and implementation differ")
    for value in (feature_cutoff, inputs_available, decision_completed, published):
        _integer(value)
    if (
        not isinstance(publication_id, str)
        or not publication_id
        or not isinstance(product_id, str)
        or not product_id
        or feature_cutoff % 5000
        or not feature_cutoff <= decision_completed <= published
        or not feature_cutoff <= inputs_available <= decision_completed
    ):
        raise ValueError(
            "identity, grid cutoff and causal processing/publication clocks required"
        )
    if max_feature_age_ms is not None:
        _integer(max_feature_age_ms)
    age = published - feature_cutoff
    within = age <= max_feature_age_ms if max_feature_age_ms is not None else None
    return PublicationTiming(
        VERSION,
        publication_id,
        product_id,
        feature_cutoff,
        inputs_available,
        decision_completed,
        published,
        age,
        max(0, inputs_available - feature_cutoff),
        published - decision_completed,
        inputs_available <= feature_cutoff,
        published != feature_cutoff or inputs_available > feature_cutoff,
        max_feature_age_ms,
        within,
        (
            "publication_age_policy_unfrozen"
            if within is None
            else (
                "feature_window_too_old"
                if not within
                else "study_and_source_gates_unfrozen"
            )
        ),
        False,
        hashlib.sha256(raw).hexdigest(),
        hashlib.sha256(
            Path(__file__).read_text(encoding="utf-8").encode("utf-8")
        ).hexdigest(),
    )

| Aggregate reader-to-writer latency | Committed frames waited locally | Lost tail is unmeasured; no failure-time ordering |
| Aggregate callback-through-commit timing | A completed journal callback was slow | Processing, SQLite, CPU scheduling and storage delay share the metric |
| Progress/after-commit hooks | Possibly long gaps between progress lines | Hook duration is absent from callback latency; no live phase at overflow |

The test pair reproduces the same old counts (257 received, zero delivered,

some timing hints, but do not uniquely identify the failure-time mechanism.

## Additive instrumentation

`QueuedSocket` retains at most 257 monotonic return timestamps. There is no
per-frame disk log or payload copy. On failed enqueue it freezes one
`instant-reader-overflow-v1` snapshot with:

- Distinct `queue_full` / `frame_bytes_exceeded` reasons, wall receipt label,
  queue capacity/depth, received/delivered counts and oldest queued-frame age.
- Counts of socket returns in the trailing 100ms and 1s, retained sample span
  and `lower_bound` flags when the bounded ring cannot prove an exact count.
  The rejected frame is included in the return count, never in the queue.
- Active owner phase (`journal_callback`, `after_commit`, or
  `between_callbacks`), phase elapsed time, batch frame count and last
  completed owner activity. `between_callbacks` includes uninstrumented
  setup, lifecycle, progress and loop work; it is not proof of an idle CPU.

The reader immediately emits one `READER_OVERFLOW {json}` line to stderr,
before the owner resumes. A completed pilot also stores the same frozen
snapshot at `REPORT.json -> reader[i].overflow`. The healthy instrumented
control has `overflow: null`. The report returns copies, so later shutdown,
writer recovery or caller mutation cannot alter failure-time evidence.
Stderr emission is best effort, not a durable journal receipt; retain it
separately if the owner never completes a final report.

Counts and queue depth are local samples around failed enqueue, not an atomic
transaction with the consumer's queue removal. Arrival windows measure local
`recv()` returns, not exchange sends or physical network ingress. Large counts
can mean exchange traffic, network/OS/socket buffering or local scheduling.
A long `journal_callback` identifies the occupied phase, not disk versus CPU
versus descheduling. A burst and a writer stall can occur together; no automatic
single-cause classifier or capacity recommendation is justified.
