# Timed volatility and activity baseline draft

The offline `build_timed_baseline(recorder, product, cutoff, asof=...)` API
takes a declared product and 5-second feature cutoff. It computes two
independent components from the timed input-evidence ledger.

- **Volatility and BTC beta.** Use 61 fresh, paired minute-close midpoints
  to form 60 log returns. The final close is
  `floor((cutoff_ms - 35000) / 60000) * 60000`. Use the coin's sample
  standard deviation and centered OLS against BTC. A flat BTC return series
  gives beta 1 with an explicit fallback flag. The trade receipt proof covers
  the preceding 5-second bucket at the first close through the final close.
- **Activity pace.** Use 120 half-open 30-second receipt windows ending at
  `cutoff_ms - 30000`. Median trade count and quote USD amount use first
  receipts. One proof chain within a single continuity epoch covers the
  full hour. A proved empty bin contributes zero to both medians. When a
  proof is missing, the component is unavailable. Maker side is irrelevant
  for these unsigned medians.

A missing benchmark close can block volatility while pace is ready. Each
ready component records the latest availability of the quote or trade
evidence it used, including the producer's payload completion. The
evidence as-of can precede the feature cutoff once a component's window
has closed. The plan must already be recorded by that as-of, and the as-of
must have elapsed on the recorder clock. The builder reads the clock again
after calculation and provenance hashing to record computation completion.
A later calculation with the same evidence as-of can use the same evidence
while having a later completion timestamp.

The result pins the inventory hash and its recorded time, the packet count
and SHA-256 of the known canonical packet prefix, and normalized source
and contract hashes. The draft is for offline component calculation.
Positive eligibility still requires feature, quote-health and publication
evidence. Throughput and availability on a real capture remain to be
measured.

The five new synthetic cases cover delayed proof completion, stable
prefix provenance, BTC quote missingness, verified empty versus unknown
activity, a trade exactly at the exclusive right boundary, and the
inventory and processing clocks. See the
[check record](results/instant_timed_baseline_20261106/CHECKS.json).

Next, define F3's book observation integration and F4's candle source and
alignment, then build cutoff-bound F1–F5 feature evidence. The data-only
pilot will measure coverage and latency after the remaining interfaces and
pilot rules are in place.

# Timed instant-radar feature draft

The offline builder computes the five candidate inputs for one declared
product, 5-second feature cutoff `b`, and direction. The sign is +1 for up
and −1 for down. Each component has its own value or missingness reason.
The formulas use the candidate's fixed 30s and 180s windows. Sigma uses
the declared 0.0015 one-minute floor. The accompanying diagnostics retain
velocity, unsigned activity, cutoff spread, expansion, and raw signed
180s return for comparisons.

| Input | Computation |
| --- | --- |
| F1 acceleration | Direction times (30s midpoint log return minus one fifth of the preceding 150s log return) divided by floored sigma. Endpoints are `b-180s`, `b-30s`, and `b`. |
| F2 signed participation | Signed taker USD imbalance times log1p of 30s USD pace relative to the timed 120-bin median. Use at least five distinct first-receipt matches in `[b-30s,b)`, known maker sides inverted to taker sides, positive USD, and a positive baseline median. |
| F3 sampled pressure | Signed time-weighted mean of `(bid_size-ask_size)/(bid_size+ask_size)` over `[b-30s,b)`. |
| F4 active compression and expansion | Directional positive 30s velocity divided by `1 + log(max(high)/min(low))/(sigma*sqrt(15))`. |
| F5 market-relative movement | Signed `(coin_180s - beta * BTC_180s) / sigma` from synchronized midpoint endpoints. Retain the beta fallback flag. |

## F3 boundaries

Use fully processed book and transport events known by the evidence as-of,
ordered by local receipt and then sequence. Carry the last known state into
the window. At a receipt timestamp the final state in that millisecond applies
to the following segment. Events at `b` are excluded. Integrate only across
segments with a valid BBO, a matching active heartbeat, a quote no older than
10s, and a heartbeat no older than 5s. Age is measured from the original
receipt. Reaching an age limit at a segment's right edge is allowed. Any
positive-duration gap makes F3 unavailable. The denominator is 30 seconds.
The result measures sampled book pressure at logger receipts.

## F4 boundaries

Build high and low from actual first-receipt matches in 15 half-open,
UTC-aligned one-minute bins. The last bin ends at
`floor((b-30s)/60000)*60000` so its trades precede the expansion window.
One receipt-ID proof chain within a single continuity epoch must cover the
15-minute window. Count certified empty minutes separately and leave their
high and low absent. Require at least ten traded minutes. An incomplete
proof makes F4 unavailable. These are receipt-time trade candles.

## Timing and scope

A result carries the observation cutoff, explicit evidence as-of, each
ready component's evidence-availability time, actual baseline and feature
computation completion, and the timed baseline's evidence-prefix provenance.
Post-cutoff receipts cannot enter the formulas. A proof that becomes known
later can verify pre-cutoff observations at an explicitly later as-of. The
result identifies computation after the cutoff as diagnostic. An input
evidence timestamp does not include the subsequent baseline or feature
calculation. A consumer must use the recorded computation completion times.
This API produces offline evidence for the separately versioned decision
and grading design. A data-only pilot still needs to measure real
coverage and latency before a decision age limit or study freeze.

The detailed candidate and its comparison plan are recorded in the
[study draft](INSTANT_STUDY_FREEZE_CANDIDATE_20261030.md) and
[methodology triage](INSTANT_METHOD_REVIEW_TRIAGE_20261106.md).

"""Compute offline baseline components from the timed input ledger."""

from decimal import Decimal, DecimalException
import math
from pathlib import Path
import statistics

from instant_coverage import _integer
from instant_input_evidence import BENCHMARK, GRID_MS, _sha


VERSION = "instant-timed-baseline-v1-draft"
MINUTE_MS = 60000
PACE_MS = 30000
RETURN_COUNT = 60
PACE_COUNT = 120


def _volatility(recorder, product, start, end, asof):
    if start < GRID_MS:
        return dict(status="unavailable", reason="insufficient_history")
    if end > asof:
        return dict(status="unavailable", reason="window_not_closed")

    availability, returns = [], {}
    for pid in dict.fromkeys((product, BENCHMARK)):
        # Include the bucket preceding the first close, as in micro_baseline.
        _, _, reason, available = recorder._trade_window(
            pid, start - GRID_MS, end, asof
        )
        if reason:
            return dict(status="unavailable", reason=reason, product_id=pid)
        availability.append(available)
        closes = []
        for cutoff in range(start, end + 1, MINUTE_MS):
            quote, available = recorder._quote_at(pid, cutoff, asof)
            if not quote["available"]:
                return dict(
                    status="unavailable",
                    reason=quote["reason"],
                    product_id=pid,
                    missing_close_ts_ms=cutoff,
                )
            bid, ask = float(quote["bid"]), float(quote["ask"])
            closes.append(math.log(bid + (ask - bid) / 2))
            availability.append(available)
        returns[pid] = [b - a for a, b in zip(closes, closes[1:])]

    coin, btc = returns[product], returns[BENCHMARK]
    coin_mean, btc_mean = statistics.fmean(coin), statistics.fmean(btc)
    variance = sum((value - btc_mean) ** 2 for value in btc)
    fallback = variance == 0
    beta = (
        1.0
        if fallback
        else (
            sum((a - coin_mean) * (b - btc_mean) for a, b in zip(coin, btc)) / variance
        )
    )
    sigma = statistics.stdev(coin)
    if not math.isfinite(beta) or not math.isfinite(sigma):
        return dict(status="unavailable", reason="nonfinite_baseline")
    return dict(
        status="ready",
        inputs_available_ts_ms=max(availability),
        return_count=len(coin),
        sigma_1m=sigma,
        beta=beta,
        beta_fallback=fallback,
    )


def _pace(recorder, product, start, end, asof):
    if start < 0:
        return dict(status="unavailable", reason="insufficient_history")
    if end > asof:
        return dict(status="unavailable", reason="window_not_closed")
    # A single certified chain covers the entire interval, including every
    # empty subwindow. Receipts are then assigned to half-open 30s bins.
    _, trades, reason, available = recorder._trade_window(product, start, end, asof)
    if reason:
        return dict(status="unavailable", reason=reason, product_id=product)
    counts, amounts = [0] * PACE_COUNT, [Decimal(0)] * PACE_COUNT
    try:
        if recorder._index is None:
            for trade in trades:
                index = (trade.receipt_ts_ms - start) // PACE_MS
                counts[index] += 1
                amounts[index] += Decimal(trade.price) * Decimal(trade.size)
        else:
            counts, amounts = recorder._index.pace_bins(product, start, end, PACE_MS)
        median = statistics.median(amounts)
    except DecimalException:
        return dict(status="unavailable", reason="invalid_quote_amount")
    if not median.is_finite():
        return dict(status="unavailable", reason="invalid_quote_amount")
    return dict(
        status="ready",
        inputs_available_ts_ms=available,
        window_count=PACE_COUNT,
        pace_trade_median_30s=statistics.median(counts),
        pace_quote_usd_median_30s=str(median),
    )


def build_timed_baseline(recorder, product, cutoff, *, asof):
    """Assess independent volatility and pace components for a declared slot.

    Windows end before cutoff, so a baseline may finish ahead of the slot.
    The evidence as-of is an input limit and completion uses the recorder clock.
    """
    _integer(cutoff)
    _integer(asof)
    if recorder.failed:
        raise ValueError("failed evidence recorder")
    if product not in recorder.products or cutoff not in recorder.cutoffs:
        raise ValueError("product/cutoff outside declared inventory")
    if asof < recorder.inventory_recorded_ts_ms:
        raise ValueError("inventory was not yet recorded at baseline evidence time")
    if asof > recorder._now():
        raise ValueError("baseline evidence time has not been reached")

    sigma_end = (cutoff - 35000) // MINUTE_MS * MINUTE_MS
    sigma_start = sigma_end - RETURN_COUNT * MINUTE_MS
    pace_end = cutoff - PACE_MS
    pace_start = pace_end - PACE_COUNT * PACE_MS
    volatility = _volatility(recorder, product, sigma_start, sigma_end, asof)
    pace = _pace(recorder, product, pace_start, pace_end, asof)
    known_packets, prefix, _ = recorder._prefix_at(asof)

    result = dict(
        version=VERSION,
        product_id=product,
        benchmark=BENCHMARK,
        feature_cutoff_ts_ms=cutoff,
        knowledge_asof_ts_ms=asof,
        sigma_start_ts_ms=sigma_start,
        sigma_end_ts_ms=sigma_end,
        pace_start_ts_ms=pace_start,
        pace_end_ts_ms=pace_end,
        volatility=volatility,
        pace=pace,
        inventory_sha256=recorder.inventory_sha256,
        inventory_recorded_ts_ms=recorder.inventory_recorded_ts_ms,
        known_packet_count=known_packets,
        known_packet_prefix_sha256=prefix,
        input_implementation_sha256=recorder.source_sha256,
        input_contract_sha256=recorder.contract_sha256,
        book_implementation_sha256=recorder.book.source_sha256,
        book_contract_sha256=recorder.book.contract_sha256,
        monitor_implementation_sha256=recorder.book.monitor.source_sha256,
        monitor_contract_sha256=recorder.book.monitor.contract_sha256,
        builder_sha256=_sha(Path(__file__).read_text(encoding="utf-8").encode("utf-8")),
        scope="offline_baseline_components",
        fitting_allowed=False,
    )
    result["computation_completed_ts_ms"] = recorder._now()
    return result

"""Offline F1–F5 evidence from declared, receipt-time observations."""

from decimal import Decimal, DecimalException
import math
from pathlib import Path

from instant_book import MAX_QUOTE_AGE_MS
from instant_coverage import MAX_HEARTBEAT_GAP_MS
from instant_input_evidence import BENCHMARK, DIRECTIONS, _sha
from instant_timed_baseline import MINUTE_MS, PACE_MS, build_timed_baseline


VERSION = "instant-timed-features-v1-draft"
SLOW_MS = 180000
SIGMA_FLOOR = 0.0015
CANDLE_COUNT = 15
MIN_TRADED_CANDLES = 10
MIN_MATCHES = 5


def _missing(reason, **details):
    return dict(status="unavailable", reason=reason, **details)


def _value(value, *known, **details):
    if not math.isfinite(value):
        return _missing("nonfinite_feature", **details)
    return dict(
        status="ready", value=value, evidence_available_ts_ms=max(known), **details
    )


def _price_point(recorder, product, cutoff, asof):
    if cutoff < 0:
        return _missing(
            "insufficient_history", product_id=product, endpoint_ts_ms=cutoff
        )
    quote, known = recorder._quote_at(product, cutoff, asof)
    if not quote["available"]:
        return _missing(quote["reason"], product_id=product, endpoint_ts_ms=cutoff)
    bid, ask = float(quote["bid"]), float(quote["ask"])
    mid = bid + (ask - bid) / 2
    return _value(math.log(mid), known, spread_bps=(ask - bid) / mid * 10000)


def _move(start, stop):
    for point in (start, stop):
        if point["status"] != "ready":
            return point
    return _value(
        stop["value"] - start["value"],
        start["evidence_available_ts_ms"],
        stop["evidence_available_ts_ms"],
    )


def _participation(recorder, product, start, end, asof, pace, sign):
    if start < 0:
        return _missing("insufficient_history"), {}
    _, trades, reason, known = recorder._trade_window(product, start, end, asof)
    if reason:
        return _missing(reason), {}

    counts = dict(verified_match_count=len(trades))
    try:
        amounts = {"buy": Decimal(0), "sell": Decimal(0), None: Decimal(0)}
        for trade in trades:
            amounts[trade.taker_side] += Decimal(trade.price) * Decimal(trade.size)
        total = sum(amounts.values())
        if not total.is_finite():
            return _missing("invalid_quote_amount"), counts
        counts["verified_quote_usd"] = str(total)
        counts["unknown_side_usd"] = str(amounts[None])
        if pace["status"] != "ready":
            return (
                _missing("pace_baseline_unavailable", baseline_reason=pace["reason"]),
                counts,
            )
        median = Decimal(pace["pace_quote_usd_median_30s"])
        if median <= 0:
            return _missing("nonpositive_pace_median"), counts
        ratio = float(total / median)
        if not math.isfinite(ratio) or (total > 0 and ratio == 0):
            return _missing("invalid_volume_pace"), counts
        counts["unsigned_volume_pace"] = ratio
        if len(trades) < MIN_MATCHES:
            return _missing("too_few_matches"), counts
        if any(trade.taker_side is None for trade in trades):
            return _missing("unknown_match_side"), counts
        if total <= 0:
            return _missing("nonpositive_window_quote_amount"), counts
        imbalance = float((amounts["buy"] - amounts["sell"]) / total)
    except DecimalException:
        return _missing("invalid_quote_amount"), counts
    return (
        _value(
            sign * imbalance * math.log1p(ratio), known, pace["inputs_available_ts_ms"]
        ),
        counts,
    )


def _book_pressure(recorder, product, start, end, asof, sign):
    if start < 0:
        return _missing("insufficient_history")
    # Each stream is already receipt ordered. Overwriting the start key keeps
    # the last carried state; overwriting a same-ms key keeps the last event.
    book_rows, transport_rows = recorder._book_window(product, start, end, asof)
    states = {max(start, state.receipt_ts_ms): state for state in book_rows}
    transport = {max(start, row["receipt_ts_ms"]): row for row in transport_rows}
    boundaries = sorted({start, end, *states, *transport})
    state = heartbeat = None
    known = end
    integral = 0.0
    for left, right in zip(boundaries, boundaries[1:]):
        state = states.get(left, state)
        heartbeat = transport.get(left, heartbeat)
        if state is None or state.best is None or state.reason:
            return _missing("book_state_unavailable", segment_start_ts_ms=left)
        if (
            heartbeat is None
            or heartbeat["reason"] != "heartbeat_observed"
            or state.connection_id != heartbeat["connection_id"]
        ):
            return _missing("heartbeat_unavailable", segment_start_ts_ms=left)
        if right - state.observation_ts_ms > MAX_QUOTE_AGE_MS:
            return _missing(
                "stale_book_span",
                first_invalid_ts_ms=max(
                    left, state.observation_ts_ms + MAX_QUOTE_AGE_MS
                ),
            )
        if right - heartbeat["receipt_ts_ms"] > MAX_HEARTBEAT_GAP_MS:
            return _missing(
                "stale_heartbeat_span",
                first_invalid_ts_ms=max(
                    left, heartbeat["receipt_ts_ms"] + MAX_HEARTBEAT_GAP_MS
                ),
            )
        bid_size, ask_size = map(Decimal, state.best[2:])
        pressure = float((bid_size - ask_size) / (bid_size + ask_size))
        integral += pressure * (right - left)
        known = max(
            known,
            recorder._packet_by_seq[state.receipt_seq].available_ts_ms,
            recorder._packet_by_seq[heartbeat["receipt_seq"]].available_ts_ms,
        )
    return _value(sign * integral / (end - start), known)


def _candle_range(recorder, product, start, end, asof):
    if start < 0:
        return _missing("insufficient_history")
    _, trades, reason, known = recorder._trade_window(product, start, end, asof)
    if reason:
        return _missing(reason)
    candles = {}
    for trade in trades:
        bucket = trade.receipt_ts_ms // MINUTE_MS
        price = Decimal(trade.price)
        low, high = candles.get(bucket, (price, price))
        candles[bucket] = min(low, price), max(high, price)
    counts = dict(
        traded_candles=len(candles), verified_empty_candles=CANDLE_COUNT - len(candles)
    )
    if len(candles) < MIN_TRADED_CANDLES:
        return _missing("too_few_traded_candles", **counts)
    low = min(candle[0] for candle in candles.values())
    high = max(candle[1] for candle in candles.values())
    try:
        log_range = float(high.ln() - low.ln())
    except DecimalException:
        return _missing("invalid_candle_range", **counts)
    return _value(log_range, known, **counts)


def _scaled_velocity(move, baseline, sigma, sign):
    if move["status"] != "ready":
        return move
    if baseline["status"] != "ready":
        return _missing(
            "volatility_baseline_unavailable", baseline_reason=baseline["reason"]
        )
    return _value(
        sign * move["value"] / sigma,
        move["evidence_available_ts_ms"],
        baseline["inputs_available_ts_ms"],
    )


def _acceleration(velocity, prior, sigma, sign):
    for component in (velocity, prior):
        if component["status"] != "ready":
            return component
    return _value(
        velocity["value"] - sign * prior["value"] / (5 * sigma),
        velocity["evidence_available_ts_ms"],
        prior["evidence_available_ts_ms"],
    )


def _compression(velocity, candles, sigma):
    for component in (velocity, candles):
        if component["status"] != "ready":
            return component
    expansion = max(velocity["value"], 0)
    return _value(
        expansion / (1 + candles["value"] / (sigma * math.sqrt(CANDLE_COUNT))),
        velocity["evidence_available_ts_ms"],
        candles["evidence_available_ts_ms"],
    )


def _market_relative(coin_move, btc_move, baseline, sigma, sign):
    for component in (coin_move, btc_move):
        if component["status"] != "ready":
            return component
    if baseline["status"] != "ready":
        return _missing(
            "volatility_baseline_unavailable", baseline_reason=baseline["reason"]
        )
    return _value(
        sign * (coin_move["value"] - baseline["beta"] * btc_move["value"]) / sigma,
        coin_move["evidence_available_ts_ms"],
        btc_move["evidence_available_ts_ms"],
        baseline["inputs_available_ts_ms"],
        beta_fallback=baseline["beta_fallback"],
    )


def build_timed_features(recorder, product, cutoff, direction, *, asof):
    """Calculate a single offline feature row with explicit evidence and clocks."""
    if direction not in DIRECTIONS:
        raise ValueError("direction must be up or down")
    if type(cutoff) is not int or type(asof) is not int or asof < cutoff:
        raise ValueError("feature as-of must be at or after the integer cutoff")
    baseline = build_timed_baseline(recorder, product, cutoff, asof=asof)
    vol, pace = baseline["volatility"], baseline["pace"]
    sigma = max(vol["sigma_1m"], SIGMA_FLOOR) if vol["status"] == "ready" else None
    sign = 1 if direction == "up" else -1
    slow, fast = cutoff - SLOW_MS, cutoff - PACE_MS

    start, middle, end = (
        _price_point(recorder, product, t, asof) for t in (slow, fast, cutoff)
    )
    recent, prior = _move(middle, end), _move(start, middle)
    coin = _move(start, end)
    btc = (
        coin
        if product == BENCHMARK
        else _move(
            _price_point(recorder, BENCHMARK, slow, asof),
            _price_point(recorder, BENCHMARK, cutoff, asof),
        )
    )
    candle_end = fast // MINUTE_MS * MINUTE_MS
    candle_start = candle_end - CANDLE_COUNT * MINUTE_MS
    candles = _candle_range(recorder, product, candle_start, candle_end, asof)
    velocity = _scaled_velocity(recent, vol, sigma, sign)
    flow, activity = _participation(recorder, product, fast, cutoff, asof, pace, sign)
    features = {
        "F1": _acceleration(velocity, prior, sigma, sign),
        "F2": flow,
        "F3": _book_pressure(recorder, product, fast, cutoff, asof, sign),
        "F4": _compression(velocity, candles, sigma),
        "F5": _market_relative(coin, btc, vol, sigma, sign),
    }

    row = dict(
        version=VERSION,
        product_id=product,
        direction=direction,
        feature_cutoff_ts_ms=cutoff,
        knowledge_asof_ts_ms=asof,
        baseline=baseline,
        sigma_used=sigma,
        candle_start_ts_ms=candle_start,
        candle_end_ts_ms=candle_end,
        features=features,
        controls=dict(
            current_velocity_sigma=velocity,
            unsigned_activity=activity,
            cutoff_spread_bps=end.get("spread_bps"),
            candle_range=candles,
            expansion=(
                max(velocity["value"], 0) if velocity["status"] == "ready" else None
            ),
            raw_signed_180s=sign * coin["value"] if coin["status"] == "ready" else None,
        ),
        builder_sha256=_sha(Path(__file__).read_text(encoding="utf-8").encode("utf-8")),
        scope="offline_feature_components",
        fitting_allowed=False,
    )
    row["computation_completed_ts_ms"] = recorder._now()
    row["later_diagnostic"] = row["computation_completed_ts_ms"] > cutoff
    return row

# Delayed decision evidence and later cutoff grading

The offline module `instant_delayed_decision.py` adds two draft record versions,
`instant-delayed-decision-v1-draft` and `instant-later-cutoff-grade-v1-draft`.

## Decision and publication clocks

`prepare_decision` builds a timed F1–F5 row for a declared slot. It freezes
canonical feature and paired anchor-quote bytes and measures preparation
completion after serialization. The snapshot retains the observation cutoff
`b`, evidence as-of, feature completion, and decision preparation completion.
All observations used in the feature values precede `b`. A later evidence
as-of can admit later proof of a pre-cutoff interval. Unavailable features
stay unavailable in that snapshot.

`observe_publication` associates the snapshot with a caller-supplied
publication identity and timestamp. It validates this chronology and
measures when the observation was recorded:

```text
b <= decision evidence as-of <= feature computation completed
  <= snapshot preparation completed <= observed publication <= observation recorded
```

The observation uses the feature computation completion as the time all
computed inputs could be available, while the feature row preserves the
individual evidence clocks. The supplied timestamp is an observation
assertion. Durable delivery and scoring are separate work. An optional
diagnostic age bound uses the existing publication helper. Study selection
of an age limit awaits measured source and processing latency.

## Later grading

`grade_publication` returns an independent diagnostic view at a later
as-of. It checks the decision's inventory, ledger prefix and source provenance,
and records the grading prefix and decision digest. The requested as-of
must have elapsed and include the publication observation. It returns
`pending` through the period before `b+H`, with `H` equal to 300 or 900
seconds. Once mature, absent or contradicted evidence returns
`unavailable` with a null outcome. A mature grade with adequate evidence
returns a Boolean `cutoff_verified_forward`.

For this draft the published snapshot needs all five features available.
Grading rechecks the frozen inputs' trade proof windows and both products'
receipt-ID coverage from the 5s bucket preceding the anchor through
the horizon. Later conflicts in these windows can invalidate a grade.
As-ofs that fall during a packet's processing are unavailable once the
horizon is mature. This conservative boundary keeps a monitor result
from reaching grading before the recorder finishes the packet. The
original snapshot and earlier evidence views remain independently
reproducible.

The outcome uses paired, fresh receipt-time midpoints at each 5s endpoint,
the frozen anchor and volatility/beta, and first-receipt matches strictly
inside `(b,b+H)`. The terminal residual, spread and sigma threshold, peak
retention and forward match floor follow the pinned V2 impulse rules.
A contradiction in the anchor quote vetoes grading. A different outcome
as-of can change the grade's evidence without substituting new decision
inputs. Publication time is reported separately from the fixed cutoff
horizon. The grade includes whether publication preceded the horizon end.

This draft measures a fixed-cutoff outcome on scoped evidence. A later
publication-anchored quote benchmark needs its own entry and maturity
rules. The recorder and publication path still need durable clock and
connection evidence before a data-only coverage and latency pilot.
